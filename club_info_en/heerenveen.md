## The stadium
The Abe Lenstra Stadion (about 27,000 places) is on the edge of Heerenveen and is named after the best-known player in the club's history. The stadium opened in 1994 and was later expanded, with four covered stands close to the pitch. It is a modern, clear stadium where you have a good view from almost every spot. Around the stadium are car parks, restaurants and a fan shop.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Heerenveen is warm, proud and Frisian. The crowd, which comes from all over Friesland, sings a lot and supports the team unconditionally. The club is the heart of the province and you feel that in a friendly but enthusiastic atmosphere. At the Frisian derby against Cambuur and in matches against the big clubs the stadium gets particularly loud.

## Getting there
The stadium is about 2 km from Heerenveen station. You walk there in a little over a quarter of an hour or take a bike. By car you can park well in the area, but it is busy on matchdays.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except against Ajax, PSV, Feyenoord and at the Frisian derby. For those matches it is wise to book early.

## The city
Heerenveen is best known for Thialf, the world-famous speed skating arena. There are also Oranjewoud, an estate with lovely walking and cycling routes, and the Frisian lakes in the area.

## Why a visit is worth it
SC Heerenveen is a club with its own character and a following that goes beyond the town limits. The stadium is modern and pleasant, the atmosphere friendly and the crowd is proud of its Frisian identity. The area also offers nature, water and speed skating history, so you can combine a match well with a visit to Thialf or a bike ride through Oranjewoud. For anyone who wants to discover the north of the Netherlands, this is a good reason.
