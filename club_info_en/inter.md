## The stadium
San Siro, officially Stadio Giuseppe Meazza (about 75,000 places), is in the west of Milan and is one of the best-known stadiums in Europe. The stadium has three tiers of steep stands and striking round stair towers on the outside. It is shared with AC Milan and has an imposing, almost monumental character. There is a museum and a stadium tour that takes you right up to the pitch. Plans are being made for a new stadium, so check the current situation.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Inter is proud and loud, with the Curva Nord as the heart of the fanatical supporters. The stadium turns blue and black and on European nights the atmosphere is impressive. The Derby della Madonnina against Milan is one of the most spectacular matches in football.

## Getting there
By metro line 5 (lilac) to San Siro Stadio, or tram 16 from the centre.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. For the derby against Milan and big nights very difficult; for other matches often more achievable. Stadium tours are a good alternative.

## The city
See Milan: the Duomo, the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, La Scala, the Castello Sforzesco and the Navigli.

## Why a visit is worth it
Inter is one of the big names in football and plays in one of the most famous stadiums in the world. The match is often spectacular and the atmosphere grand. Milan offers fashion, art, architecture and excellent restaurants, so you can make a complete city trip of it. Anyone who wants to see San Siro before it changes would do well not to wait too long.
