## The stadium
San Mamés (about 53,000 places) opened in 2013 on the site of the old stadium and stands in the middle of Bilbao, within walking distance of the centre. The stadium is called "La Catedral" and has a fully covered, steep layout with a striking illuminated facade in red and white. The stands sit almost vertically above the pitch, which amplifies the sound enormously. There is a stadium tour and a club museum where you can discover the history of Basque football.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Athletic is one of the best in Spain: the supporters sing, wave flags and the stadium trembles at key moments. On matchdays the streets around the stadium are coloured red and white and full of supporters eating pintxos and drinking beer. You experience directly how strongly the club is tied to the region.

## Getting there
San Mamés metro station is right by the stadium; from the centre you can also walk there in about 15 minutes. The airport is about 12 km outside the city.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Many places are for members, so it is not easy; for the derby with Real Sociedad, and against Madrid and Barcelona, it is very difficult.

## The city
The Guggenheim Museum, the Casco Viejo with the Zazpi Kaleak, pintxos, the Puente Colgante in Portugalete and the Artxanda funicular.

## Why a visit is worth it
Athletic Club is unique in the football world for its policy of fielding only Basque players, and you see that in the passion of the crowd. San Mamés is one of the most beautiful and impressive stadiums in Europe, in the middle of a city that also has a lot to offer. Bilbao combines art, architecture and gastronomy in a way few cities match. Anyone who wants to see a match with real identity should not miss this one.
