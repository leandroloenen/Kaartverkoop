## The stadium
The Gewiss Stadium (about 25,000 places) is on the edge of Bergamo and has been extensively renovated in recent years, with new stands close to the pitch. The stadium is compact and modern and has an intimate atmosphere. It lies against the hills of the Lombard Pre-Alps. The club's black and blue colours are everywhere.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Atalanta is passionate and proud: the Curva Nord sings throughout the match and the crowd is proud of what the club has achieved. The stadium is small, so you can feel the sound. Before and after the match it is lively in the streets around the stadium.

## Getting there
Bergamo is about an hour from Milan by train. The stadium is about 3 km from the station; buses run there. Milan Bergamo airport is close by.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Very difficult: the stadium is small and demand is high.

## The city
The Città Alta (upper town) with Venetian walls (UNESCO), the Piazza Vecchia, the Cappella Colleoni and the funicular.

## Why a visit is worth it
Atalanta is one of the biggest surprises of European football, with attacking play and a loyal following. The stadium is special and the atmosphere intense. Bergamo is a beautiful city with a medieval upper town, close to Milan and Lake Como. Tickets are hard to get, but worth the effort for an authentic Italian match.
