## The stadium
The Millerntor-Stadion is in the heart of Hamburg, close to the Reeperbahn and the harbour. It is a compact stadium where you sit close to the pitch, in the middle of the city. Tickets are often scarce, especially for big matches. Check the club in good time for the sales moments and for which tickets are for members only.

## The matchday experience
St. Pauli is more than a football club: it is a distinctive fan culture with the skull and crossbones as its symbol and a clear social stance. The atmosphere in the stadium is loud, creative and friendly. The derby against HSV is one of the most special matches in Germany.
