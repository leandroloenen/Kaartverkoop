## The stadium
Stadion Galgenwaard (about 23,750 places) is on the east side of Utrecht, in a green area on the edge of the city. The stadium has been expanded in phases and has four covered stands close to the pitch, so the sound hangs well. You see the club's red and white colours everywhere. There are a fan shop and enough food and drink outlets, so you can spend a few hours there before kick-off.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Galgenwaard is warm, proud and fanatical. The crowd sings a lot and the supporters' section behind the goal provides flags, singing and choreographies. In matches against Ajax, Feyenoord and PSV, and in the derbies against clubs from the region, the tension runs high. It remains a friendly matchday, even for first-time visitors.

## Getting there
The stadium is about 3 km from Utrecht Centraal. You take the bus, cycle or walk in a little over half an hour. By car you can park nearby, but it is busy on matchdays.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Many places are for season ticket holders, but single tickets are easy to get for most matches. Against the big opponents you need to be quick.

## The city
Utrecht has the Domtoren (about 112 metres), the Oudegracht with its wharves, the Centraal Museum, the Rietveld Schröder House and the Railway Museum. The centre is compact and full of terraces.

## Why a visit is worth it
FC Utrecht is a club with a loyal following and a stadium where you sit close to the match. The atmosphere is warm and matchday is easy to organise, because Utrecht is in the middle of the country and easy to reach from everywhere. The city itself is one of the nicest city centres in the Netherlands, with canals, wharves and a pleasant nightlife. Anyone who wants to combine an Eredivisie match with a day in a beautiful city is well placed here.
