## The stadium
The Etihad Stadium (about 53,000 places, expanding to some 60,000) is in the east of Manchester, in a large sports complex with training pitches and an academy. It is a modern stadium with steep stands and a striking round roof. Around the stadium are a fan zone, a shop and plenty of places to eat and drink. The stadium was originally built for the 2002 Commonwealth Games.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at City is cheerful, proud and ever bigger, with a crowd that supports its team enthusiastically. The level of football is very high and you often see attractive, dominant play. Before and after the match it is busy in the fan zone and the surrounding pubs.

## Getting there
By tram to Etihad Campus, in about 10 minutes from Manchester Piccadilly. You can also walk in about 25 minutes.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Difficult, but usually easier than at United. A membership improves your chances.

## The city
The Science and Industry Museum, the Northern Quarter, the National Football Museum, the Town Hall and Salford Quays. Manchester is a city of music and football.

## Why a visit is worth it
Manchester City has been one of the best teams in Europe for years and you see football at the highest level. The stadium is modern and easy to reach. Manchester is also one of the nicest cities in England, with music, football history, museums and a busy nightlife. Anyone who wants to see the best of current football can go here, even though it is not easy to get tickets.
