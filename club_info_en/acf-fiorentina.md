## The stadium
Stadio Artemio Franchi (about 43,000 places) is in the Campo di Marte district, east of the centre of Florence. The stadium was designed by Pier Luigi Nervi and is known for its distinctive concrete structure, with the famous spiral staircase and the cantilevered roof. It is being renovated, so check the current capacity and any restrictions. It is an architectural monument and one of the most beautiful stadiums in Italy.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Fiorentina is proud, purple and passionate: the Curva Fiesole sings throughout the match and the crowd turns the stadium violet. The supporters are proud of their city and their style of play. After the match you can walk calmly back to the centre.

## Getting there
The stadium is in Campo di Marte, about 3 km from the centre. Take a bus or tram (lines 2 and 3 are nearby), or the train to Firenze Campo di Marte.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually achievable, except for big clubs.

## The city
The Duomo, the Uffizi, the Ponte Vecchio, the Galleria dell'Accademia (David) and the Piazzale Michelangelo. Pisa and Siena are day trips.

## Why a visit is worth it
Fiorentina combines football with one of the most beautiful cities in the world. The stadium is an architectural monument and the atmosphere is special. Florence offers the Uffizi, the Duomo, the Ponte Vecchio and excellent food. Tickets are generally achievable, so you can easily put together a complete culture and football trip.
