## The stadium
The Emirates Stadium (about 60,000 places) opened in 2006 in Holloway, in north London, and replaced the old Highbury. It is a modern stadium with steep stands close to the pitch and a roof that holds the sound in. Around the stadium are statues of club icons, and there is a museum and shop. Highbury itself has been converted into flats, but the memory lives on.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Arsenal is proud, critical and has become steadily louder. The crowd sings a lot and the stadium is almost always sold out. Before the match the pubs around the stadium are worth a visit, and the walk from the tube station gives you the matchday feeling straight away.

## Getting there
Arsenal tube station (Piccadilly Line) or Finsbury Park (Victoria Line and Piccadilly Line), from where it is about a 10 minute walk. Holloway Road is also an option.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Very difficult: members and season ticket holders come first. A stadium tour is easier.

## The city
London: Tower Bridge, the British Museum, the Tower of London, Camden Market and Hyde Park.

## Why a visit is worth it
Arsenal play attractive football in a modern stadium that is easy to reach from central London. The atmosphere is big and the standard is high. London also offers everything a city trip needs: museums, parks, history and nightlife. Anyone who wants to see Premier League football in a world city can go to Arsenal, even though tickets are hard to get.
