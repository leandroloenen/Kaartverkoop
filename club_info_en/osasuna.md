## The stadium
Estadio El Sadar (about 23,500 places) is on the southern edge of Pamplona and is a round, steep stadium surrounded by hills. The stands are high and sit close to the pitch, so the sound stays inside. The stadium is a classic that has been modernised in recent years, but has kept its robust character. Osasuna's red colours dominate the view.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere in El Sadar is fierce, loud and intimidating for opponents. The crowd, with the Indar Gorri as the best-known supporters' group, sings and jumps throughout the match. Those who take on the challenge get a raw and genuine Spanish stadium experience with plenty of passion and few frills.

## Getting there
The stadium is about 3 km southeast of the centre. You can get there by bus or taxi, or walk in a little over half an hour.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. For most matches tickets are available, but against Real Madrid, Barcelona and Athletic Club it is difficult.

## The city
Pamplona is best known for the San Fermín bull runs in July, which Hemingway made famous. There are also the old town, the Plaza del Castillo, the Ciudadela and the city walls.

## Why a visit is worth it
Osasuna offers one of the most authentic matchday experiences in La Liga, with a fierce atmosphere in a stadium that has kept its character. The club is proud of its region and you notice that in everything. Pamplona itself has a charming old town, a rich food culture and of course the famous San Fermín festivities. Anyone who wants to experience the real Spain away from the big tourist destinations is in the right place here.
