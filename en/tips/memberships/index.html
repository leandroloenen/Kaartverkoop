10-memberships

Fans who want to see a big club just once often look at a reseller first. That is convenient, especially if the club itself is sold out, but for top matches the price there is usually higher than the official price. There is another route many supporters overlook: a club membership.

The idea

Many clubs give members priority in ticket sales. For the most popular matches, that is often the only realistic way to get a ticket from the club. A basic membership is relatively cheap, and it gives you access to the regular sale at the official ticket price.

An example: a Tottenham Hotspur membership costs around £45. With it you can try to buy tickets for games against clubs such as Manchester City or Manchester United. At a reseller the price for such matches is often at least twice as high, although it differs per match. Even after paying for the membership you may come out cheaper. If it does not work out through the club, a reseller remains a logical place to look next.

An important caveat

A membership is not a guarantee. For top matches there are far more members than tickets. Clubs therefore use sale windows per group, ballots, loyalty points or a cap on tickets per member. You need to be on time, know the rules and sometimes be lucky. Always check which members get priority for which match: it differs by club and by membership level. Also check the current price and conditions on the official site.

Sharing the cost of a membership

Do you go to the same club regularly? Then you can share the cost with friends or acquaintances. This works best for clubs just across the border from the Netherlands, such as Borussia Dortmund, Schalke 04, Bayer Leverkusen or Borussia Mönchengladbach. You split the cost and improve the odds that someone secures a ticket, even against bigger opponents for which general sale is rarely realistic.

Mind the terms, though: memberships are usually personal, and tickets are normally issued in a name. Some clubs check this at the gate. So only share in a way that fits the club's rules, for example by each taking out your own membership and planning together, or by agreeing who tries to order at which moment.

When is it worth it?

  • You want to attend a specific top match and prices at a reseller are considerably above face value.
  • You go to the same club several times a season.
  • You are willing to keep an eye on the sale dates.
