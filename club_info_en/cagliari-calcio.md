## The stadium
The Unipol Domus (about 16,000 places) is on the edge of Cagliari, near the Sant'Elia district, close to the harbour and the sea. The stadium opened in 2017 on the site of the old stadium and is modern, compact and covered. The stands are close to the pitch and the stadium is easy to reach from the centre. The club's red and blue colours dominate.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Cagliari is proud, warm and island-friendly. The crowd sings a lot and is proud of its Sardinian identity. The stadium is small, so you sit close to the action and the mood is friendly and involved.

## Getting there
Cagliari can only be reached by plane or boat. Elmas airport is about 6 km from the city. From the centre you walk or take a bus to the stadium.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except for Inter, Juventus or Milan.

## The city
The Castello district, the Poetto beach, the Bastione di Saint Remy, the Roman amphitheatre and Sardinian cuisine, for example culurgiones and porceddu.

## Why a visit is worth it
Cagliari offers football, beach and island atmosphere in a single trip. The stadium is compact and tickets are generally easy to get. The city has a beautiful old quarter, a long beach and its own cuisine. Anyone combining a football weekend with sun and sea is in the right place here.
