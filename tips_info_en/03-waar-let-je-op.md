Before you click "pay", it is worth pausing to think about a few things. These are the points that most often go wrong or lead to disappointment.

## Is this the official shop?

Go to the ticket site through the club's own website, or via the link on this site. If you use a search engine, look carefully at the address and watch out for ads. There are sites that look official but are not. If you end up on a page with much higher prices than you expected, go back and check the official source.

## What does it really cost?

Look at the total price, including service fees and shipping costs. At some clubs a club card or membership comes on top of that. Also factor in travel costs, especially for a match abroad.

## Is the ticket in your name?

Many clubs issue tickets in a name. The name on the ticket must then match your ID. Also bear in mind that reselling is not always allowed, or only through the club's official platform.

## Which section are you buying?

Check whether you are buying a seat with the home fans or with the visiting supporters. In some stadiums the sections are strictly separated. Ask yourself whether you would rather sit neutral or right in the heart of the atmosphere.

## What are the stadium rules?

Read the rules on bags, flags, cameras and payment methods beforehand. Many stadiums now work with payment cards instead of cash and only allow small bags.

## Is the match date final?

The kick-off can still change, for example because of television or European matches. So book travel and hotel with a free-cancellation option if possible, as long as the time is not fixed.

## Short checklist before you pay

- Official club site?
- Checked the total price including fees?
- Right match, date and section?
- Details in your name correct?
- Do you know how you will receive the ticket?
