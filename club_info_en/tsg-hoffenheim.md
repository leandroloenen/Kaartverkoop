## The stadium
The PreZero Arena (about 30,000 places) is on the edge of Sinsheim, right beside the motorway, in an open landscape. The stadium is modern and compact, with covered stands and a good view from every spot. Next to the stadium is the Technik Museum, where you can see a Concorde, among other things. The surroundings are simple and practical, with plenty of parking.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Hoffenheim is relaxed and friendly, with many families and a crowd that comes mainly for the football. It is less fanatical than at the traditional clubs, but there is plenty of room to enjoy things quietly. The organisation is professional and matchday goes pleasantly.

## Getting there
By S-Bahn S5 from Heidelberg or Mannheim to Sinsheim Museum/Arena, about 30 to 40 minutes. On matchdays there are extra trains.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get.

## The city
Sinsheim is small, but the Technik Museum Sinsheim has a Concorde and a Tupolev Tu-144. Combine the visit with Heidelberg: the castle, the Alte Brücke and the Philosophenweg.

## Why a visit is worth it
TSG Hoffenheim is a remarkable example of a small club that grew to Bundesliga level. The stadium is modern and the football is often attacking. Sinsheim itself is small, but the Technik Museum and the proximity of Heidelberg make it a nice day trip. For anyone who wants to combine a quiet, cheap match with a visit to a beautiful university city, this is a good choice.
