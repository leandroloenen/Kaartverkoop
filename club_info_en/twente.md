## The stadium
The Grolsch Veste (about 30,000 places) opened in 1998 on the west side of Enschede, on the edge of the city. It is a modern stadium with four steep, covered stands close to the pitch, so the sound hangs well. Inside it is clear and comfortable, and the club's red colours dominate the view. Around the stadium there is plenty of space for parking and food and drink, and there is a fan shop.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at FC Twente is passionate and proud, with a loyal crowd from all over Twente and the Achterhoek. The supporters' section behind the goal sings throughout the match and at bigger matches the stadium is a seething mass of red. It is a typical eastern Dutch matchday: down to earth, friendly and full of passion.

## Getting there
The stadium is about 3 km from the centre and the station of Enschede. You take the bus or a bike; on matchdays there are extra services. By car you park at the stadium, but expect crowds.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except against the big clubs such as Ajax, PSV and Feyenoord, and in the derby against Heracles. Book in good time then.

## The city
Enschede has the Oude Markt with its many terraces, the Rijksmuseum Twenthe, Museum TwentseWelle and the Volkspark. The German border and the beautiful Twente countryside are close by.

## Why a visit is worth it
FC Twente is the big club of the east of the Netherlands and you notice that in the following and the atmosphere. The stadium is modern, comfortable and full of sound, without being overwhelmingly big. Enschede is a pleasant city with a lively Oude Markt and plenty of greenery in the surroundings. Anyone who wants to get to know the east of the Netherlands and see a good match would do well to visit De Grolsch Veste.
