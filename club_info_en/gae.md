## The stadium
The Adelaarshorst (about 10,000 places) is one of the oldest and most characteristic stadiums in the Netherlands and sits in the middle of a residential area in Deventer. The stands are right next to the pitch, so you can almost smell the match. The stadium has an old-fashioned, classic character with steep stands and little luxury. That is exactly its charm: it feels like football as it used to be.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Go Ahead Eagles is passionate and close to the pitch, and the supporters sing almost throughout the match. Because the stadium is in the middle of the neighbourhood, supporters stream into the stadium from all sides, which creates a pleasant bustle. A match at De Adelaarshorst feels more like a neighbourhood party than a stadium visit.

## Getting there
From Deventer station you walk there in about 20 minutes, or take a bike or bus. Parking nearby is difficult.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. The stadium is small and many matches are sold out, certainly after the cup success. Be quick.

## The city
Deventer is a Hanseatic city with a beautiful centre: the Brink, the Waag, the Lebuïnus Church and the Bergkwartier. Well known are the Deventer koek (spiced cake) and the largest book market in Europe, held every year in early August.

## Why a visit is worth it
Go Ahead Eagles offers one of the most authentic matchday experiences in the Netherlands. The stadium is small, the atmosphere intense and the bond between club and city is tangible. Deventer itself is a beautiful Hanseatic city with a pleasant centre, good restaurants and terraces on the IJssel. You can easily combine an afternoon in town with an afternoon or evening of football here, and you feel part of the club immediately.
