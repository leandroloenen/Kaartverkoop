## The stadium
The Volkswagen Arena is in Wolfsburg, next to the Volkswagen site and the Allerpark. It is a modern stadium with a clear view from the stands. Wolfsburg is not a big football city: matchday revolves around the club and the area around the factory. Check the club for the sales moments and expect a calm, well-organised day.

## The matchday experience
VfL Wolfsburg, the Wolves, draws a mixed crowd of loyal fans and visitors from the region. The atmosphere is less fanatical than at the big German clubs, but the stadium is easy to reach and comfortable. The city is about an hour from Hanover and Braunschweig.
