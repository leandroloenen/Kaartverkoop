## The stadium
Villa Park (about 42,000 places) is in Aston, in the north of Birmingham, and is one of the most beautiful classic stadiums in England. The Holte End is the famous stand behind one goal and the main stand has a special, historic facade. The stadium sits in the middle of a residential area, with streets of brick houses around it. You feel the history in every corner.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Villa Park is proud and loud: the Holte End sings throughout the match and the crowd is demanding. On European nights the stadium is one of the most atmospheric in England. Before the match the pubs in the area are full of supporters in claret and blue.

## Getting there
Take the train from Birmingham New Street to Witton, about 10 minutes, right next to the stadium. Buses also run there.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Very difficult: almost all season tickets are taken. For a cup tie there is sometimes a better chance.

## The city
Birmingham: the Bullring, the Jewellery Quarter, the Library of Birmingham, the canals and the Balti Triangle. The Black Country Living Museum is also nearby.

## Why a visit is worth it
Aston Villa is one of England's historic top clubs and plays in a stadium with plenty of character. The match is often exciting and the atmosphere is big. Birmingham is a varied city with museums, canals and a special food culture. Anyone who wants to see a classic English stadium with a rich tradition is in the right place at Villa, although tickets are hard to get.
