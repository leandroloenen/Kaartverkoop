## The stadium
Stamford Bridge (about 40,000 places) is in west London, in the Fulham district, and is surrounded by a hotel, flats and shops. The stadium has four covered stands close to the pitch. There is a club museum, a stadium tour and a large fan shop. There are plans for a larger rebuild or new stadium, so check the current situation.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Chelsea is proud and varies: at top matches the stadium is loud and fierce, at other matches quieter. The crowd expects a lot and shows it. Before the match supporters in blue walk through the streets and the pubs fill up.

## Getting there
Take the tube to Fulham Broadway (District Line), from where it is about a 5 minute walk. West Brompton and Imperial Wharf stations are alternatives.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Very difficult: season tickets are taken and memberships come first. A tour is a good alternative.

## The city
London: Tower Bridge, the British Museum, Big Ben, Hyde Park and the King's Road, close to the stadium.

## Why a visit is worth it
Chelsea is one of the most successful clubs of recent decades and plays in the heart of London. The stadium is easy to reach and the match is often of top quality. Around the stadium lie Chelsea, Kensington and the King's Road, with plenty of shops and restaurants. Anyone who wants to combine top football with a city trip is in the right place.
