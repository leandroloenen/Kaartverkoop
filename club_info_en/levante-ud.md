## The stadium
Estadi Ciutat de València (about 26,000 places) is Levante's home ground and sits in a residential area of Valencia. It is a classic, intimate stadium with stands close to the pitch and an old-fashioned character. The club's blue and red colours can be seen throughout the neighbourhood. The stadium feels like a real neighbourhood ground, far removed from the big, modern arenas.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Levante is friendly and down to earth, with a loyal following that stays with its club through thick and thin. The crowd sings a lot and the mood in the stadium is warm and personal. At the derby against Valencia the stadium is completely full and the tension grows enormously.

## Getting there
The stadium is in the city of Valencia and can be reached by bus or metro. Check the current route on the official club site. From the centre it is about 20 to 30 minutes.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except for the derby and the big clubs.

## The city
See Valencia CF for the city itself: the City of Arts and Sciences, the Mercado Central, La Lonja, the Turia gardens and Malvarrosa beach.

## Why a visit is worth it
Levante offers a different, more local side of Valencia and a real neighbourhood club experience. Tickets are affordable, the crowd is friendly and you sit close to the pitch. Because you are staying in Valencia, you can effortlessly combine the match with beach, old town and paella. Anyone who prefers to see a small club rather than the big name will find exactly what they are looking for here.
