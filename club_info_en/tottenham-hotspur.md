## The stadium
The Tottenham Hotspur Stadium (about 62,000 places) opened in 2019 and is one of the most modern stadiums in the world. It has a retractable pitch beneath which lies an artificial pitch for NFL games, and behind the southern goal stands the largest single-tier stand in the United Kingdom. There is its own brewery, a club shop and you can walk over the roof and abseil via a special attraction. The stadium is designed to attract visitors outside matches too.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Spurs is modern and pleasant, with an enormous south stand that sings together and brings the stadium to life. Before the match there are plenty of bars and places to eat in and around the stadium. The crowd is demanding and the stadium has a high level of comfort.

## Getting there
Take the Overground to White Hart Lane, or the tube (Victoria Line) to Seven Sisters or Tottenham Hale, from where you walk about 10 minutes.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Very difficult: season tickets are taken and demand is high. For European nights or cup matches there is sometimes a better chance.

## The city
London: Tower Bridge, the British Museum and Camden Market.

## Why a visit is worth it
Tottenham Hotspur has one of the most impressive stadiums in the world, with every facility you could wish for. The match is usually attractive and the stadium is easy to reach from central London. London also offers endless sights. Anyone who wants to see a modern stadium and combine it with a city trip has an excellent choice here.
