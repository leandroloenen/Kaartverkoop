Anyone looking for tickets for a sold-out match often ends up on sites that resell tickets. They are easy to find and often have tickets for matches that are sold out at the club. Here is what is good to know before you order.

## What does a reseller ticket cost?

Resellers offer tickets they bought themselves or took over from others, and add costs on top. For popular matches the price is therefore higher than the club's price. At some matches the difference is small, at others it is large.

## What should you look out for?

- Check whether the ticket is in a name and whether it is valid for you.
- See whether the club allows resale. Some clubs only allow it through their own platform.
- Choose a party that protects buyers, for example with a guarantee if tickets are not delivered.
- Check what happens if the match is cancelled or moved.
- Compare the total price, including service fees.

In some countries there are also legal rules on reselling tickets above face value. So read the terms of the club and the site.

## What are the official alternatives?

- **The club's official ticket site.** There is often a later sale phase for members or for everyone.
- **The club's official resale platform.** Many clubs have their own ticket exchange, where season ticket holders and other buyers can pass on their ticket at the original price.
- **A sign-up or waiting list.** Some clubs send a message when tickets become available.
- **The box office on matchday.** Sometimes tickets are still available just before kick-off. Do not count on it, but it can happen.

## Choosing a reseller

If the club is sold out and the official channels do not work out, a reseller can be a good option. Check that the ticket fits the club's rules, compare the total price and choose a party that protects buyers. Preferably pay with a method that lets you claim your money back if something goes wrong.

A personal experience: I have bought tickets through P1 Travel myself, for a Tottenham Hotspur match and a West Ham United match. That worked well for me.
