## The stadium
The WWK Arena (about 30,000 places) is on the southern edge of Augsburg and opened in 2009. It is a modern and compact stadium with covered stands and a good view. The surroundings are practical and clear, with plenty of parking and a tram stop close by. It is not a spectacular design, but the stadium is comfortable and functional.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Augsburg is down to earth, warm and friendly. The crowd sings, but it is less fanatical than at the big clubs. You sit comfortably, have a good view and can fully concentrate on the match.

## Getting there
Take tram line 3 from the Hbf to the WWK Arena, in about 20 minutes. There is also a dedicated stop on matchdays.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except against Bayern München (the Bavarian derby).

## The city
The Fuggerei, the oldest social housing project in the world (1521), the Rathaus with the Golden Hall, the Maximilianstraße and the Augsburger Puppenkiste. Augsburg is more than 2,000 years old.

## Why a visit is worth it
FC Augsburg offers a relaxed and affordable way to see Bundesliga football. The stadium is modern, tickets are generally easy to get and the match is often exciting. Augsburg itself is a historic city with remarkable sights such as the Fuggerei, and lies close to Munich. For anyone who wants to combine a match with a second city in Bavaria, this is a smart choice.
