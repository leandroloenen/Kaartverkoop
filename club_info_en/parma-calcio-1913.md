## The stadium
The Stadio Ennio Tardini (about 27,000 places) is in the middle of Parma, within walking distance of the station and the centre. It is a classic Italian stadium with an English-style layout, where the stands are close to the pitch. It is one of the oldest stadiums in Italy and has a low, intimate look. The club's yellow and blue colours are everywhere.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Parma is proud, friendly and hospitable. The crowd sings a lot and lives with the club, which has blossomed again after a difficult period. It is a pleasant and accessible matchday.

## Getting there
From Parma station you walk about 15 minutes to the stadium. The city lies between Milan and Bologna on the high-speed line.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get.

## The city
The Duomo, the Baptistery, the Teatro Regio, the Palazzo della Pilotta and the food: Parmigiano Reggiano and prosciutto di Parma. Parma is a UNESCO Creative City of Gastronomy.

## Why a visit is worth it
Parma combines football with one of the best culinary cities in Italy. The stadium is in the centre, the atmosphere warm and tickets are generally easy to get. The city has a beautiful Duomo, a fine theatre and excellent ham and cheese. Anyone who loves food and football will find the ideal combination here.
