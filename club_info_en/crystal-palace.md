## The stadium
Selhurst Park is in South London, in the Selhurst area, and is a stadium with an old-fashioned, friendly character. The stands are close to the pitch and matchday feels British and traditional. You need a membership or season ticket for every ticket. Check the club for the sales moments, because the priority periods are tightly scheduled.

## The matchday experience
Crystal Palace has one of the loudest home crowds in London, with the Holmesdale Fanatics at the heart of the atmosphere. The mood is warm and proud, with lots of singing and flags. Combine your visit with a day in South London.
