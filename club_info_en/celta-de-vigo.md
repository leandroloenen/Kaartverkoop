## The stadium
Abanca-Balaídos (about 24,500 places) is Celta's home ground and sits in a residential area on the edge of Vigo. It is a classic stadium that has been modernised in recent years, with steep stands close to the pitch. The sky-blue colour is visible everywhere, from the seats to the flags. The stadium has an intimate feel and suits the scale of the city.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere in Balaídos is warm, loud and passionate, with a crowd that supports its team unconditionally. The stadium is not too big, so you sit close to the action and the singing hangs in the air throughout the match. Before and after the match you can go to the bars nearby, where it is often lively and loud.

## Getting there
The stadium is about 3 km from the centre. You take a bus or taxi, or walk in about 30 minutes. Vigo airport is about 10 km from the centre.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Tickets are usually easy to get, except against Real Madrid, Barcelona and other top matches.

## The city
Vigo is the largest city in Galicia. See the old town O Berbés, the Castro castle with a view over the bay, Samil beach and the Cíes Islands (in summer). Eat the fresh oysters.

## Why a visit is worth it
Celta de Vigo is a likeable club with attractive football and a crowd that loves the club. The stadium is friendly, ticket prices are reasonable and you sit close to the pitch. Vigo itself is a port city with beautiful views, good beaches nearby and world-famous seafood. It is a good base for a trip around Galicia, and the match is a nice addition.
