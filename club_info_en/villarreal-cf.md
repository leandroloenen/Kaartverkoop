## The stadium
Estadio de la Cerámica (about 23,000 places) is Villarreal's home ground and sits in the middle of Vila-real, close to the centre and the station. The stadium is built in the club's yellow colours and the name refers to the region's tile industry. The stands sit close to the pitch and the stadium is modern and comfortable. Around the stadium it is friendly and welcoming.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Villarreal is friendly and easygoing: a content and proud crowd in a small town, far from the big football circuses. On European nights the stadium is full and yellow, and in matches against the big clubs it gets loud. Everything feels small, manageable and personal.

## Getting there
Vila-real is a small town north of Valencia. Trains run regularly from Valencia (about an hour) and Castelló. From the station you walk to the stadium.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. The stadium is small, but tickets are usually easy to get, except against Real Madrid and Barcelona.

## The city
Vila-real itself is quiet and known for its tile industry. Combine the visit with Castelló de la Plana or the beaches of the Costa del Azahar, or with Valencia.

## Why a visit is worth it
Villarreal is an example of how a small town can produce a big club. The stadium is compact and pleasant and tickets are generally affordable and easy to get. You see attractive football and can combine the visit with a day at the beach or a trip to Valencia. Anyone who wants to taste Spanish football culture without the crowds of the big cities chooses a relaxed alternative here.
