## The stadium
The Stadio Olimpico Grande Torino (about 28,000 places) is in the south of Turin and was originally built for the university games of the 1930s. The stadium has an oval layout with an athletics track and a low, wide roof. The Curva Maratona behind one goal is the heart of the fanatical supporters. The stadium has a modest, old-fashioned character and bears the name of the legendary team.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Torino is emotional, proud and loyal: the supporters sing throughout the match and keep the memory of the past alive. On 4 May the Superga disaster is commemorated every year. At the city derby against Juventus the stadium is a seething red-maroon festival.

## Getting there
Take a tram or bus from the centre, about 30 minutes, or walk from Porta Nuova in about 40 minutes.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except for the city derby against Juventus.

## The city
The Mole Antonelliana, the Egyptian Museum, the Royal Palace, the Piazza San Carlo and the Superga hill with a memorial site. Try the bicerin, a Turin coffee with chocolate.

## Why a visit is worth it
Torino is a club with a deep, emotional bond with its supporters and its past. The stadium is classic and the atmosphere genuine. Tickets are generally easy to get and the match is affordable. Turin itself is an elegant city with an Egyptian museum, mountain views and excellent coffee. For anyone who wants to see an authentic Italian match, this is an excellent choice.
