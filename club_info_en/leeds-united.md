## The stadium
Elland Road (about 37,000 places) is south of the centre of Leeds and is a classic English stadium with tall, steep stands. The Kop, the East Stand and other stands are close to the pitch, and the sound hangs in the stadium. The stadium has a rough, tough look and the club's white colours are everywhere. In front of the entrance is a statue of a club icon.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Elland Road is intense and proud: the crowd sings "Marching On Together" and is often at the stadium hours before the match. The crowd is demanding and full of passion, and the stadium is one of the loudest in England. For many supporters it is an almost religious experience.

## Getting there
From Leeds station you walk in about 30 minutes, or take a bus or shuttle on matchdays.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Very difficult: almost all season tickets are sold and the waiting list is long.

## The city
The Victorian shopping arcades, the Royal Armouries, Kirkstall Abbey and the buzzing nightlife district. The Yorkshire Dales are close by.

## Why a visit is worth it
Leeds United has one of the most fanatical followings in England and plays in a stadium full of atmosphere. The match is intense and the sense of togetherness is great. Leeds is also a buzzing city with shopping arcades, museums and a busy nightlife. Anyone who wants to experience raw English football should be here, although it is hard to get tickets.
