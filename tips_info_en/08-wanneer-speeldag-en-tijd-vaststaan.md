Before the season starts, the fixture list is published: who plays whom, when, and who is at home. But that only tells you which weekend (or round) a match falls in, not whether it will be played on Friday, Saturday, Sunday or Monday, or at what time. In many countries the final match day and kick-off time only follow weeks or months later, and it works differently everywhere. For your trip, flight or hotel that is important to know.

## Why it is set later

There are a few standard reasons why a match can still move:
- **Television.** Broadcasters choose which matches they want to show live, and the day and time are adjusted to that.
- **European matches.** Clubs playing in the Champions League, Europa League or Conference League need rest between matches. How far a club goes therefore partly decides when its league matches are played.
- **Cups.** A club that goes far in a national cup may see a league match moved.
- **Cities and police.** Events in the city or the availability of police can play a role.

## The Netherlands: Eredivisie and Eerste Divisie

The competition programme is finalised in the summer; for the 2025/26 season that was on 24 June. It takes into account the wishes of clubs, municipalities, supporters and ESPN. After that, a lot can still change, and the KNVB states per period what is still provisional. In 2025/26 it went like this:
- Rounds 5 to 12 could still change, depending on how the Dutch clubs did in Europe. The decision followed in early September.
- Rounds 13 to 22 were finalised in early September.
- Rounds 23 onwards are not finalised before the end of December.

That means that for a match in the second half of the season you often have to wait a long time for the final day and time. The exact dates differ per season.

## England: Premier League and Championship

The Premier League publishes the whole calendar in advance, but always subject to change. By default, weekend matches are scheduled for Saturday at 15:00, and midweek matches around 20:00. For each round, about five matches are picked for live broadcast on UK television, and these can be played from Friday evening to Monday evening. The league, the broadcasters and the local authorities confirm those changes about six weeks before each round before January, and about five weeks after. Because live broadcasts on Saturdays between 14:45 and 17:15 are not allowed in the United Kingdom, 15:00 matches stay off screen.

Here too, matches move for European rounds and cups, especially the FA Cup. A match involving a club in Europe is only moved after UEFA has confirmed the European dates.

In the Championship, the EFL picks the television matches in blocks of a few months at a time. Part of the season is then already known, and the next block follows. How far ahead exactly differs per season.

## Germany: Bundesliga and 2. Bundesliga

In the summer the German league publishes the calendar; in 2026/27 that was on 2 July. The exact kick-off times are then set in blocks of several rounds. The first blocks follow shortly after publication in the summer, and later blocks are only determined during the season, among other things after the European draw is known. The league therefore deliberately waits for the European competitions before planning further. Usual slots are Friday evening, Saturday afternoon, Saturday evening (often the top match) and Sunday. We have not found a fixed deadline by which the time has to be known.

## Italy: Serie A

In Italy it is mainly the rounds that are fixed for the season. The day and kick-off time are announced in blocks, which get smaller towards the end of the season. In 2023/24, for example, the first block with rounds 1 to 4 followed shortly after the calendar, rounds 5 to 19 in early September and rounds 20 to 29 at the end of December, then increasingly smaller blocks. Each round has fixed slots from Saturday to Monday: Saturday around 15:00, 18:00 and 20:45, Sunday around 12:30, 15:00, 18:00 and 20:45, and sometimes Monday at 20:45. The exact slots and blocks differ per season.

## France: Ligue 1

In France the broadcasters first choose which matches they want to show, and then the league confirms the whole programme. That normally comes about 30 days before the round. At the start of the season the league makes a provisional overview of the announcement dates, but that is an indication and can still change because of European matches and the cup. Sometimes part of a round is announced first and the rest a few days later. Matches are played on Friday, Saturday and Sunday, and for 2026/27, according to reports, the slots include Friday 20:45, Saturday 17:15 and 20:45 and Sunday 15:00, 17:15 and 20:45.

## Spain: La Liga and Segunda División

LaLiga announces the day and kick-off time per round, for all matches at once, and the times are then fixed for the whole weekend. We have not been able to establish how many weeks ahead that happens, so do not assume a fixed period. In 2026/27 the times of the first three rounds were announced about a month before the start. In the last two rounds, matches that matter for the title, European places or relegation are played at the same time. Always check the time on LaLiga's official site before you book anything.

## European competitions

The Champions League, Europa League and Conference League have their own schedule. The league phase programme follows shortly after the draw, and the kick-off time is usually 18:45 or 21:00 Central European Time, sometimes earlier. For a club in Europe, a league match around the European days can move.

## What does this mean for your trip?

- Book flights and hotel preferably with a cancellation option, or wait until day and time are final. This applies especially to matches later in the season.
- If our overview only shows a date and no time yet, the kick-off time is not yet known. As soon as the club or competition announces it, we update it.
- Ticket sales can start before the final time is known. So check the day and time once more after buying.
