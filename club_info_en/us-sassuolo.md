## The stadium
The Mapei Stadium – Città del Tricolore (about 21,500 places) is in Reggio Emilia, not in Sassuolo itself, and is one of the most modern small stadiums in Italy. It has four covered stands close to the pitch and a cheerful, green and black look. The stadium is owned by the club and is part of a sports complex. The surroundings are spacious and practical.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Sassuolo is relaxed and friendly, with many families and a crowd that comes mainly for the football. It is less fanatical than at the big clubs, but the match is pleasant and the organisation good. You sit comfortably and have a good view.

## Getting there
Take the train to Reggio Emilia (from Bologna or Milan) and then a bus or taxi, about 3 km. On matchdays there are sometimes shuttles. Modena is also close by.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get.

## The city
Reggio Emilia has the Piazza Prampolini and the Basilica della Ghiara. Modena (Ferrari museum, balsamic vinegar) and Maranello are close by.

## Why a visit is worth it
Sassuolo is a remarkable story of a small club in the top division and offers a quiet and affordable match. The Emilia-Romagna region is the cradle of Italian food, cars and music, with Modena and Maranello around the corner. So you combine football with balsamic vinegar, Ferrari and local pasta. For anyone who loves cars and food, this is a great trip.
