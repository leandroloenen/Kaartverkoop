I love going to football matches, in the Netherlands and abroad. A full stadium, the supporters, the city around it: it is a great experience every time. But before you get there, you first have to get hold of tickets. And that turns out to work differently at every club.

## Every club does it differently

One stadium sells first to season ticket holders, the next to members, a third requires a club card, and yet another puts all matches on sale at once. On top of that, the information about when which group can buy tickets sits in a different place on every website. Working it all out takes quite some time, especially if you have several matches or several countries on your list.

## Everything in one place

I wanted to make that easier. On this site you can find, per club and per match, when sales start and for whom. I have also added some extra information per club about the stadium, the matchday experience, how to get there and the city. That way you can choose faster which match you would like to go to, and you know right away when to be alert.

## Straight from the club

There are plenty of resellers and other intermediaries offering match tickets. Those tickets are often a lot more expensive, and not everyone wants or can afford to pay that for a match. Many clubs simply sell tickets themselves, at face value. But many people do not know where or how. Hence this initiative: to make buying from the club itself easier.

## A personal project

This site is a hobby project by a football fan and not an official channel of any club. I do my best to keep everything as accurate as possible, but always check sale times on the club's official ticket site. Spot a mistake, or miss a club? Let me know.
