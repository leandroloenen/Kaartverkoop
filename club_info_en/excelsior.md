## The stadium
The Van Donge & De Roo Stadion (about 4,500 places), also known as Woudestein, is in the Kralingen district, in the east of Rotterdam, among greenery and the campus of Erasmus University. It is one of the smallest stadiums in the Eredivisie, with four stands right next to the pitch. You sit so close to the play that you can hear players and coaches. The stadium is simple and cosy, without frills.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Excelsior is intimate, friendly and down to earth. The crowd is small but loyal, the red and black colours are everywhere and the supporters sing along. It is a matchday for those who love football on a small scale: lots of families, lots of familiar faces and no crowds.

## Getting there
The stadium is about 5 km from Rotterdam Centraal. You take the metro to Kralingse Zoom and walk or cycle the last stretch. There is parking at the university, but it can be busy on matchdays.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. The stadium is small, so tickets for the bigger matches, such as against Feyenoord, Ajax or PSV, sell out quickly. For other matches it is usually quite doable.

## The city
Kralingen has the Kralingse Plas, a large recreational lake with beaches and walking paths. The centre of Rotterdam, with the Markthal, the Erasmus Bridge and the Cube Houses, is about 15 minutes away by metro.

## Why a visit is worth it
Excelsior offers a quiet and affordable match in a stadium of a kind you can count on one hand in the Netherlands. The atmosphere is friendly, you sit close to the pitch and tickets are usually easy to get. Rotterdam is close, so you can effortlessly combine the match with a walk around the Kralingse Plas and a visit to the centre. Anyone who wants to see football the way it used to be will find it here.
