## The stadium
The Bingoal Stadion (about 15,000 places) is in the Zuiderpark, a large city park in the southwest of The Hague. It is a compact, classic stadium with stands close to the pitch and a clear layout. Thanks to the green surroundings the stadium looks friendly and accessible, and it is not so big that you feel lost. It feels more like a neighbourhood stadium than a modern arena.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at ADO is raw, loyal and typically Hague: the crowd sings along loudly and feels every moment with the team, even when things go badly. The supporters behind the goal create a real football atmosphere. You can tell the club is deeply rooted in the city, and that makes a visit special for anyone looking for real Dutch football.

## Getting there
The stadium is in the southwest of the city. From Den Haag Centraal or Hollands Spoor take a tram or bus to the Zuiderpark; allow 20 to 30 minutes. You can park nearby, but it is busy on matchdays.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Tickets are usually easy to get, except for matches against the bigger clubs or important derbies. Book in good time then.

## The city
The Hague has the Binnenhof, the Mauritshuis (with Girl with a Pearl Earring), the Peace Palace, Panorama Mesdag and Madurodam. You are quickly at the beach of Scheveningen with its pier and boulevard.

## Why a visit is worth it
ADO Den Haag is a club with a large, fanatical following and its own distinctive identity. A match is often easy to attend, affordable and full of atmosphere. The Hague itself has a lot to offer: the seat of government, top museums, and the sea and beach of Scheveningen all lie close together. So you can soak up culture during the day and watch football in the afternoon or evening. For anyone looking for a more authentic matchday experience than at the big clubs, this is recommended.
