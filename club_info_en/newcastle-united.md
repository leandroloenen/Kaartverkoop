## The stadium
St James' Park (about 52,000 places) stands in the middle of the centre of Newcastle and dominates the skyline. The stadium has been expanded step by step and has a tall, steep layout, which amplifies the sound enormously. You walk into the stadium from the street, in the middle of the city, which gives a special city feel. From certain spots you have a view over the city.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Newcastle is overwhelming: the black and white stripes are everywhere and the crowd sings, roars and lives every moment. The Toon Army is known as one of the most loyal followings in England. Before the match the pubs around the stadium are full, and the feeling that the whole city is watching is tangible.

## Getting there
From Newcastle Central Station you walk to the stadium in about 10 minutes.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Very difficult: there is a long waiting list for season tickets. Hospitality or a tour are alternatives.

## The city
The Tyne Bridge, the Quayside, the Baltic and Sage Gateshead, the Angel of the North and the coast at Tynemouth. Hadrian's Wall is nearby.

## Why a visit is worth it
Newcastle United is one of the most passionate clubs in England and plays in a stadium in the middle of the city. The atmosphere is grand and the crowd extremely loyal. The city is also pleasant, with a beautiful waterfront, pubs, museums and the coast close by. Anyone who wants the ultimate English football feeling cannot go wrong with Newcastle, although tickets are hard to get.
