## The stadium
San Siro, officially Stadio Giuseppe Meazza (about 75,000 places), is AC Milan's home stadium and is shared with Inter. The stadium is in the west of Milan and has a distinctive look with its red steel beams and round stair towers. It has three tiers of steep stands close to the pitch. Next to the stadium is a museum where you can discover the history of both clubs. There are plans for a new stadium.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Milan is proud, loud and colourful: the Curva Sud sings throughout the match and the stadium turns red and black. On European nights the stadium is one of the most impressive in football. The crowd knows its football and appreciates good play.

## Getting there
Take metro line 5 (lilac) to San Siro Stadio, or tram 16. About 20 minutes from the centre. Metro M1 to Lotto also works, with a walk.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. The derby and big matches are very difficult; other matches are more achievable. There is also a stadium tour with a museum.

## The city
The Duomo, the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, La Scala, Leonardo's Last Supper, the Castello Sforzesco and the Navigli.

## Why a visit is worth it
AC Milan is one of the most legendary clubs in Europe and plays in a stadium full of history. The match is usually of a high standard and the stadium alone is a sight to see. Milan is also a city of fashion, design and culture, with the Last Supper and the Duomo. For anyone who wants to see top Italian football, Milan belongs at the top of the list.
