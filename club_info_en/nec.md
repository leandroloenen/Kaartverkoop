## The stadium
Stadion De Goffert (about 12,500 places) is in the Goffertpark, a large city park on the east side of Nijmegen. It is a compact stadium where the stands sit close to the pitch and where you have a good view from almost every spot. The area around the stadium is green and low on traffic. That gives matchday a relaxed, friendly look.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at De Goffert is raw and enthusiastic and the crowd sings throughout the match for the "Eendracht". Because the stadium is small, the tension at an important match is immediately in the air. A match there is intimate, the crowd sits close together and you quickly feel part of the experience.

## Getting there
The stadium is a few kilometres from Nijmegen Centraal. You take the bus or walk in about half an hour. There is limited parking, so public transport or a bike is wiser.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. The stadium is small and interest is high, so many matches sell out quickly, especially those against Ajax, PSV and Feyenoord. Look early.

## The city
Nijmegen is the oldest city in the Netherlands. See the Waalkade, the Valkhof, the Sint-Stevenskerk, the Kronenburgerpark and the Waalbrug. In July there is a party during the Vierdaagse (Four Days Marches).

## Why a visit is worth it
N.E.C. is a club with a strong identity of its own in one of the nicest cities in the Netherlands. The stadium is small and atmospheric, the supporters are proud of their club and matchday feels local and involved. Nijmegen has a pleasant centre, a beautiful waterfront and plenty of student life, so you can quickly make a complete weekend of it. For anyone who wants to experience football in a small-scale setting, De Goffert is an excellent choice.
