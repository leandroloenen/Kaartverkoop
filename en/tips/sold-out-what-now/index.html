Sold out? How to improve your chances of a ticket

A sold-out match is not always the end. There are ways you can still get a ticket, without having to turn to expensive resellers.

See whether another sale phase is coming

Many clubs sell in phases. If a match shows as "sold out" for your group, a later phase may follow for members or for everyone. Check the club page on this site or the club's ticket site to see which phases are still to come.

Use the official resale platform

Many clubs have their own ticket exchange. Ticket holders who cannot go offer their ticket there, often at the original price. Check regularly, including on matchday, as tickets are added right up to kick-off.

Sign up for a notification

Some clubs have a button such as "Notify me" or "Let me know". It sends you an email as soon as tickets become available. It costs nothing and you can always unsubscribe later.

Consider another match

If the top match is gone, look at another home match of the same club or at a club in the same city. The atmosphere at a quieter match is often at least as good. For less popular matches you usually have more choice and a better chance of a ticket closer to kick-off.

Be prepared next time

  • Put the start of the sale in your calendar.
  • Create an account in advance and log in.
  • Arrange a membership or club card if the club asks for one.
  • Be there a few minutes before the start.
