Buying a football ticket from the club itself works in broadly the same way at almost every club, even though the details differ. These steps will usually get you a long way.

## Step 1: find out when sales start

Check this site, or the club's ticket page, to see when sales for your match start and for which group. Many clubs begin with season ticket holders, then members, and only then general sale. Put a reminder in your calendar, because popular matches can sell out quickly.

## Step 2: create an account in advance

Almost every club uses a ticket account. Create yours well before the sale, confirm your email address and check that you can log in. When sales open, you do not want to lose time on a password you have forgotten.

## Step 3: check whether you need a membership or club card

Some clubs only sell tickets to members or to people with a club card or fan card. This is usually stated on the ticket page. Sometimes you can still take out a membership just before the sale, but sometimes you need to sign up weeks in advance. Read [more about the different audiences](/en/tips/sales-phases-explained/).

## Step 4: be on time at the start

Go to the ticket shop a few minutes before the start. For busy matches there may be a queue. Do not close the page or keep refreshing it, as you may lose your place.

## Step 5: choose your seat and check the details

Check the stand, the section and the number of tickets. See whether there is a maximum per person and whether tickets are issued in a name. Also check whether you are buying for the home end or the away end.

## Step 6: pay and keep your tickets

Pay through the official shop. You will usually receive the tickets as an e-ticket, in the club's app or as a PDF. Save them on your phone well in advance and make sure you can also open them without internet. Bring ID to be safe.
