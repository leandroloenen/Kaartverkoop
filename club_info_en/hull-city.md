## The stadium
The MKM Stadium (about 25,000 places) opened in 2002 and is in West Park, on the west side of Hull. The stadium is modern and comfortable, with covered stands and a good view from every spot. It is also used by a rugby club, which gives an interesting mix of supporters. The atmosphere around the stadium is friendly and practical.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Hull City is friendly, proud and loud, with a following that stays loyal to its club. The crowd sings a lot and the black and amber colours are everywhere. It is a typical northern English matchday with pubs, pies and plenty of humour.

## Getting there
From Hull Paragon station it is about a 25 minute walk or a short bus or taxi ride.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except for the bigger matches.

## The city
The old town, The Deep (aquarium), the Maritime Museum, Wilberforce House and the Humber Bridge. Hull was UK City of Culture in 2017.

## Why a visit is worth it
Hull City offers an affordable and authentic English football experience in a city that tourists often skip. The stadium is modern, tickets are generally easy to get and the people are warm. Hull has a remarkable harbour, museums and a rich history. For anyone who wants to see the real north of England, this is a good choice.
