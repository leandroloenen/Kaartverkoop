## The stadium
The RheinEnergieStadion (about 50,000 places) is in Müngersdorf, in the west of Cologne, in a green sports park. The stadium has a modern design with steep stands and a roof that holds the sound in. The colours red and white dominate, and before the match there are plenty of places to eat and drink. The stadium is one of the largest in Germany and always has plenty of atmosphere.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Köln is exuberant, cheerful and extremely loyal: the crowd sings carnival-like songs and keeps supporting its team, even in adversity. The city lives with the club, and on matchdays you see red and white scarves everywhere. It is a festive, family atmosphere.

## Getting there
By Stadtbahn line 1 from Neumarkt (towards Weiden) to Müngersdorfer Stadion, in about 15 minutes. From Köln Hbf it is about 25 minutes.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Demand is very high and many tickets go to members, so it is difficult.

## The city
Cologne Cathedral (UNESCO), the Rhine promenade, the Hohenzollern Bridge, the Chocolate Museum and the Altstadt with Kölsch breweries. Carnival begins on 11 November.

## Why a visit is worth it
1. FC Köln has one of the most beautiful and loyal supporters' cultures in Germany, in a large stadium with plenty of atmosphere. Cologne is also a beautiful city with a world-famous cathedral, a pleasant centre and its own beers. Tickets are hard to get, but achievable with planning. Anyone who wants to combine football with a pleasant German city has an excellent choice here.
