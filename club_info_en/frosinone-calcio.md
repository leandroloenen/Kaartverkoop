## The stadium
The Stadio Benito Stirpe (about 16,000 places) is on the edge of Frosinone and is a modern, compact stadium with covered stands. The stands are close to the pitch and the stadium has a good view from every spot. The club's yellow and blue colours are visible everywhere. The stadium is practical, clear and suits the scale of the town.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Frosinone is friendly, proud and local. The crowd knows each other and the match feels like a community gathering. You sit close to the pitch and the organisation is simple and friendly.

## Getting there
Frosinone is about 1 to 1.5 hours southeast of Rome by train. From the station you take a bus or taxi to the stadium.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get.

## The city
Frosinone has a small old centre with a view over the Ciociaria. Visit the Casamari Abbey and the medieval town of Anagni nearby.

## Why a visit is worth it
Frosinone offers a quiet and affordable match off the tourist track. The stadium is small and friendly and tickets are usually easy to get. The Ciociaria region has beautiful villages, abbeys and views over the hills, and Rome is an hour's journey away. Anyone who wants to see the real Italy will find an authentic day here.
