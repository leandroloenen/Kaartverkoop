Privacy policy

Last updated on Saturday 14 November 2026

Who are we?

Football Ticket Scout is operated by Leandro Loenen. For questions about privacy you can contact us via leandro_loenen@hotmail.com.

Which data do we process?

On this site you cannot create an account or fill in forms. We therefore do not ask you for personal data.

  • Settings on your own device. Your browser stores your choice about cookies on your own device. This is not sent to us.
  • Visitor statistics (only with consent). If you click "Accept", we use Google Analytics to see which pages are visited. Google places cookies for this (_ga and _ga_…) and processes data such as your IP address, device and browser data, an approximate location and the pages you view. In our reports we only see summarised figures and no IP addresses. If you click "Decline", Google Analytics is not loaded and we do not measure anything.
  • Hosting. This site is served through GitHub Pages. To show the site, GitHub processes technical data, such as your IP address, in its server logs. Our domain is managed at Cloudflare.

For what purpose and on what legal basis?

  • Visitor statistics: to understand how the site is used and to improve it. Legal basis: your consent.
  • Showing the site (hosting): the legal basis is our legitimate interest in being able to offer the site.

How long do we keep data?

We keep Google Analytics data for a maximum of 2 months. After that it is deleted automatically.

Sharing and transfers

Google and GitHub are based in the United States. Data may therefore be processed outside the European Economic Area. This happens on the basis of safeguards recognised by the European Commission, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. We do not sell data and do not share it with anyone other than those named here.

Your rights

You may ask for access to, correction or deletion of the data we hold about you, and object to processing. Because we do not keep personal data ourselves, Google carries out the request for Analytics. You can withdraw your consent at any time via Cookie settings. Do you have a complaint? Please contact us, or file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Links to other sites

We link to the ticket sites of clubs. The privacy policy of the club in question applies to those sites.
