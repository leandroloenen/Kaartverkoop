## The stadium
The Johan Cruijff ArenA (about 54,000 places) opened in 1996 and is named after the greatest player in the club's history. The stadium has a retractable roof and four steep stands, and is also used for concerts and large events. Next to the stadium you will find the Ajax Museum, and there are stadium tours that take you through the dressing rooms and the players' tunnel, among other things. The stadium is in an area with plenty of restaurants and shops, so you can spend a whole day there.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere in the ArenA is grand and loud, with the fanatical F-Side section on the north side. On European nights and in matches against Feyenoord and PSV it is one of the best atmospheres the Netherlands has to offer. Expect lots of singing, flags and a crowd that knows the club and demands attacking football.

## Getting there
The stadium is in Amsterdam Zuidoost. From Amsterdam Centraal you take the metro or train to Amsterdam Bijlmer ArenA station, which is right next to the stadium. You are there in about 20 minutes.

## Buying tickets
They are scarce. Almost all season tickets are sold, and for the bigger matches it is very hard. Look at the official sale, the club's resale and hospitality and travel packages. Against a smaller opponent you have a better chance.

## The city
Amsterdam has the canal ring, the Anne Frank House, the Rijksmuseum, the Van Gogh Museum, the Vondelpark and the Jordaan. Expect a busy tourist programme.

## Why a visit is worth it
Ajax is a club with an enormous reputation: the way of playing, the youth academy and the big European nights have put Dutch football on the map worldwide. The stadium is large, modern and easy to reach, and you can nicely add a tour and the museum to your visit. Amsterdam is also one of the most popular city trips in Europe, so you combine football with culture, food and nightlife. Anyone who wants to experience one Dutch match with an international feel usually chooses Ajax.
