## The stadium
Stadio Brianteo (about 16,000 places) is on the edge of Monza and is a compact stadium with four stands close to the pitch. The stadium has been modernised and offers a good view from every spot. The club's red and white colours are everywhere. It is in a quiet area, not far from the famous Formula 1 circuit.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Monza is friendly, relaxed and local. The crowd is proud of its club and supports the team with singing. The match feels like a pleasant family afternoon and you can calmly enjoy the football.

## Getting there
Monza is about 15 to 20 minutes from Milano Centrale by train. From Monza station it is about 2 km, by bus or on foot.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except for Inter, Milan and Juventus.

## The city
Autodromo Nazionale Monza (Formula 1), the Villa Reale with its large park, the Duomo with the Iron Crown of Lombardy.

## Why a visit is worth it
Monza offers a quiet and affordable way to see Serie A, close to Milan. The stadium is small and the atmosphere easygoing. The town has a beautiful royal park with a villa and of course the circuit, a place of pilgrimage for motor racing fans. If you want to combine football and motorsport, this is a special destination.
