## The stadium
The Stadio Olimpico (about 70,000 places) is in the Foro Italico, in the north of Rome, on the bank of the Tiber. The stadium was built for the 1960 Olympic Games and has a large, oval layout with an athletics track around the pitch. It is shared with Lazio. The stands are further from the pitch than at pure football stadiums, but the atmosphere makes up for it. Around the stadium are sports fields and parks.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Roma is passionate, emotional and red and yellow: the Curva Sud sings throughout the match and the crowd lives every moment. The supporters have a deep bond with their club and with players such as Totti. Before the match the surroundings fill with supporters eating, drinking and singing.

## Getting there
Take metro line A to Flaminio, then tram 2 or a walk. On matchdays there are extra buses. Allow 30 to 40 minutes.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. For the Derby della Capitale and the big matches very difficult; for other matches often achievable. ID is required.

## The city
The Colosseum, the Roman Forum, the Pantheon, the Trevi Fountain, the Vatican and the Trastevere district.

## Why a visit is worth it
AS Roma offers one of the most passionate matchday experiences in Italy in a city that is itself a museum. The atmosphere in the stadium is intense, the supporters are loyal and the bond with the city is tangible. Rome has the Colosseum, the Forum, the Vatican and excellent restaurants. Anyone who wants to combine a football match with one of the most beautiful cities in Europe chooses Roma.
