## The stadium
The Red Bull Arena (about 47,000 places) is built within the ramparts of the old Zentralstadion and is close to the centre of Leipzig. The outside combines historic concrete ramparts with a modern roof, which gives it a distinctive look. Inside, the stands are steep and close to the pitch, with a good view from every spot. The stadium is modern and comfortable, and lies next to a large park area.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at RB Leipzig is modern and energetic, with a crowd that sings enthusiastically and loves to see attacking football. The club is controversial because of its business model, but the local supporters are proud of their team. It is a well-organised matchday with plenty of facilities.

## Getting there
From the Hauptbahnhof you walk there in about 20 minutes; a tram also runs. Leipzig is easy to reach by train.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually achievable, but difficult for Bayern, Dortmund and European heavyweights.

## The city
The Thomaskirche (Bach), the Nikolaikirche (centre of the 1989 peaceful revolution), Auerbachs Keller, the Monument to the Battle of the Nations and the Spinnerei art complex.

## Why a visit is worth it
RB Leipzig represents modern German football, with attractive play and a modern stadium. Leipzig is one of the nicest cities in eastern Germany, with musical history, alternative districts and a young crowd. Combining a match with a city trip is easy, because everything is close together. Anyone who wants to see the latest developments in the Bundesliga has a good reason here.
