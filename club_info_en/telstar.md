## The stadium
The BUKO Stadion is in Velsen-Zuid, in the IJmuiden area, not far from Haarlem and the coast. It is a compact stadium where you sit close to the pitch. Because of its small size it fills up quickly, and tickets for matches against the bigger clubs are not always easy to get. Plan your visit in good time and check the club for the sales moments.

## The matchday experience
Telstar, the White Lions, is a club with a close, friendly atmosphere. You are near the players and the crowd, and matchday feels personal and relaxed. A visit combines well with a day at the coast or in Haarlem.
