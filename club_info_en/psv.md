## The stadium
The Philips Stadion (about 35,000 places) is in the middle of Eindhoven on the Frederiklaan, right next to the centre, among buildings. The stadium has been expanded several times over the years and has steep stands close to the pitch. It is so central that you walk there from the station. At the stadium you will find a large fan shop and several places to eat and drink, and you see the club's red and white colours throughout the city.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere in the Philips Stadion is intense and almost always good, with a crowd that sings along and lives every moment. The stadium is built very compactly, so the sound is tangible. On European nights and in matches against Ajax the experience is at its peak.

## Getting there
From Eindhoven Centraal you walk there in about 10 to 15 minutes. By car, parking is difficult; preferably use a park and ride or public transport.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Season tickets are largely sold out, so single tickets are scarce. For matches against Ajax, Feyenoord and European heavyweights you have little chance. Choose a smaller opponent or look at the official resale.

## The city
Eindhoven is a city of design and technology. Visit the Van Abbemuseum, the Philips Museum or Strijp-S, the old Philips factory site which is now a creative district. For nightlife go to Stratumseind. In October the Dutch Design Week takes place.

## Why a visit is worth it
PSV is one of the biggest clubs in the Netherlands and plays in a stadium in the middle of the city, something you rarely find in the Netherlands. The match is usually of a high standard, the atmosphere big and the crowd loyal. Eindhoven is a modern city with design, technology and a lively nightlife, so you can quickly make a nice weekend of it. For anyone who wants to combine a Dutch top match with a compact city trip, it is hard to choose better.
