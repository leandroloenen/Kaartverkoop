## The stadium
Stadion An der Alten Försterei (about 22,000 places) sits in the woods in Berlin's Köpenick district, on the east side of the city. Most places are standing room and the stadium was largely renovated by the supporters themselves. The stands are close to the pitch and the whole thing feels like a big forest theatre. It is one of the most unique stadiums in Europe, with an unrivalled community spirit.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Union is special: the supporters sing throughout the match and stand behind their team as one community. There are no commercial distractions, everything revolves around the club and the match. Around the holidays there is the famous Christmas carol singing in the stadium, a unique tradition.

## Getting there
Take the S3 to Köpenick or Wuhlheide. From Wuhlheide it is about a 15 to 20 minute walk through the woods. Allow plenty of time.

## Buying tickets
Very difficult: almost all places go to members and season ticket holders. Check the official sale.

## The city
Berlin: the Brandenburg Gate, the Reichstag, Museum Island, the East Side Gallery and the Holocaust Memorial.

## Why a visit is worth it
Union Berlin has one of the most special club cultures in Europe, built by and for the supporters. The stadium in the woods is unique and the atmosphere is intense and warm. Tickets are hard to get, but the effort is definitely worth it. Berlin itself offers history, art and nightlife, so you can combine the match with a full city trip.
