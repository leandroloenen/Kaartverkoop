## The stadium
Estadio de Mendizorrotza (about 19,800 places) is on the edge of the centre of Vitoria-Gasteiz, next to a large sports complex and a park. The stadium is a classic, almost old-fashioned ground where the stands sit close to the pitch. The atmosphere is personal and there is little between you and the pitch. The club's blue and white colours are visible throughout the area.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Alavés is warm, proud and remarkably friendly: the crowd sings a lot and supports its team even when things go badly. Because the club is not among the biggest, it feels more like a local celebration than a big spectacle. Before and after the match you can eat pintxos in the city's bars, which is part of Basque culture.

## Getting there
Vitoria-Gasteiz is about an hour's drive from Bilbao. The stadium is about a 20 minute walk from the city centre.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except against the top clubs.

## The city
Vitoria was European Green Capital in 2012. Visit the old town, Santa María Cathedral, the Plaza de la Virgen Blanca, the Artium museum and the Salburua nature reserve.

## Why a visit is worth it
Deportivo Alavés offers a quiet, affordable and friendly matchday experience in a part of Spain that tourists often skip. The stadium is small and atmospheric and tickets are generally easy to get. Vitoria-Gasteiz is a green, comfortable city with a beautiful old town and an excellent cuisine. It is a good addition to a Basque trip via Bilbao and San Sebastián, and a tip for anyone who wants to avoid the crowds.
