## The stadium
Sparta Stadion Het Kasteel (about 11,000 places) is in the Spangen district, in the west of Rotterdam, and is one of the oldest stadiums in the Netherlands. The name comes from the characteristic facade with towers, which is reminiscent of a castle. The stadium has been modernised, but has kept its classic character, with stands right next to the pitch. It is in the middle of a residential area, so you experience the neighbourhood and the club as a whole.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Sparta is intimate, proud and down to earth. The crowd sits close to the pitch and sings a lot, and the supporters are aware of the long history of the oldest professional club in the Netherlands. The red and white colours are everywhere. It feels like a real neighbourhood club, with plenty of singing and few frills.

## Getting there
The stadium is about 3 km west of Rotterdam Centraal. You take a tram or metro and walk the last stretch, or you cycle. Parking nearby is difficult.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. The stadium is small, so tickets for the bigger matches, such as against Feyenoord, Ajax and PSV, are scarce. For other matches it is usually achievable.

## The city
Rotterdam: the Erasmus Bridge, the Markthal, the Cube Houses, the Euromast and the historic Delfshaven, which is close to the stadium.

## Why a visit is worth it
Sparta offers one of the most authentic matchday experiences in the Netherlands, in a historic stadium in the middle of a working-class neighbourhood. The club has a rich past and a loyal following, and the match is often affordable and pleasant. Delfshaven and the rest of Rotterdam are close by, so you can quickly make a complete day of it. Anyone looking for a small-scale alternative to De Kuip will find it here.
