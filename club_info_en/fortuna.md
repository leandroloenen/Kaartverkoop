## The stadium
The Fortuna Sittard Stadion (about 12,500 places) is on the edge of Sittard, in the Limburg Western Mining Region. It is a compact, classic stadium with covered stands close to the pitch and a clear layout. The club's yellow and green colours are visible everywhere. The stadium is not big, but offers a good view and a friendly atmosphere around the match.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Fortuna is warm, Limburgish and friendly. The crowd often knows each other and sings a lot, and the match feels more like a community gathering than a big spectacle. In the Limburg derby against VVV-Venlo, Roda JC or MVV the tension does rise considerably.

## Getting there
The stadium is about 2 to 3 km from Sittard station. You walk there in about half an hour or take a bus or taxi. By car you can park well nearby.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except against Ajax, PSV and Feyenoord. For those matches it is wise to look early.

## The city
Sittard has a pleasant old centre with the Markt, the Basilica of the Sacred Heart and many terraces. Maastricht, Roermond and the German city of Aachen are within half an hour's drive.

## Why a visit is worth it
Fortuna Sittard is a small, likeable club with a friendly atmosphere and affordable tickets. The stadium is easy to find your way around and the crowd is proud of its Limburg identity. Sittard has a beautiful old centre and is close to Maastricht and Aachen, so you can easily combine the match with a weekend in South Limburg. For anyone who wants to explore the south of the Netherlands, this is a pleasant reason.
