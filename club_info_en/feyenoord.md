## The stadium
De Kuip (officially Stadion Feijenoord, about 47,500 places) opened in 1937 and is in the south of Rotterdam, in the Feijenoord district. The stadium is known for the steep, fully covered stands that lie like a bowl around the pitch and for the spiral staircases on the outside. It is a monumental and special stadium, often seen as one of the most beautiful in Europe. Besides matches there are a fan shop and tours, during which you see the dressing rooms and the players' tunnel, among other things.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere in De Kuip is among the best in the Netherlands: the crowd sings throughout the match, the stands tremble and before kick-off "You'll Never Walk Alone" rings through the stadium. The supporters' section is fanatical and the bond between club and city is tangible. On European nights and at the Klassieker against Ajax the stadium goes wild.

## Getting there
The stadium is south of the Maas. From Rotterdam Centraal you take a tram or metro and walk the last stretch; on matchdays there is also a dedicated railway station, Rotterdam Stadion. By car, parking nearby is difficult and expensive.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Almost all season tickets are sold and there is a waiting list, so single tickets are scarce. For the Klassieker and European heavyweights it is very difficult. A tour of De Kuip is a good alternative.

## The city
Rotterdam has the Erasmus Bridge, the Markthal, the Cube Houses, the Euromast, the Maritime Museum and the historic Delfshaven. It is a modern port city with an excellent food and nightlife scene.

## Why a visit is worth it
Feyenoord is a club with a large, loyal following and a stadium that has no equal in the Netherlands. De Kuip is a monument where the history of football is tangible, and the atmosphere is raw, loud and honest. Rotterdam is also a city with plenty of architecture, harbour atmosphere and a buzzing hospitality scene, so you can easily combine the match with a weekend in the city. Anyone who wants to experience one real Dutch football night chooses Feyenoord.
