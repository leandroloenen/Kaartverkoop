## The stadium
The Stadio Pierluigi Penzo (about 11,000 places) is on the island of Sant'Elena, on the east side of Venice, and is one of the few stadiums in the world you reach by boat. The stadium is small and classic, with stands close to the pitch and a view of the lagoon. It is one of the oldest stadiums in Italy and has a special atmosphere. Around the stadium it is quiet and green, far from the tourist crowds.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Venezia is special: you travel to the stadium by water bus and walk through the park with the supporters. The crowd is proud, loyal and friendly. The match feels like an outing, in a setting you will find nowhere else.

## Getting there
Take the vaporetto (water bus) to the Sant'Elena stop, about 20 to 30 minutes from San Marco or the Ferrovia. You can also walk, through the Giardini.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. The stadium is small, but tickets are usually easy to get, except against Inter, Juventus and Milan.

## The city
St Mark's Square, the Doge's Palace, the Rialto Bridge, the Grand Canal and the islands of Murano and Burano.

## Why a visit is worth it
Venezia offers one of the most unique football trips in the world: a match in a stadium on an island, reached by boat. The club is small and friendly and tickets are generally easy to get. Venice itself hardly needs explaining, with St Mark's Square, the canals and the islands. Anyone who wants to combine football with a dream city cannot do better.
