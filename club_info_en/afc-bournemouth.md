## The stadium
The Vitality Stadium (Dean Court, about 11,300 places) is one of the smallest stadiums in the Premier League and sits in Kings Park, among trees and residential streets. The stands are right next to the pitch and the stadium is compact and friendly. The club's red and black colours are everywhere. The stadium feels like a village ground in the top division.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Bournemouth is friendly, welcoming and proud. Because the stadium is small, you sit close to the pitch and hear every chant. The crowd is happy with the success and enjoys every match.

## Getting there
The stadium is about 2 km from the centre, in Kings Park. From the station it is about a 40 minute walk; buses run there. From London it is about 2 hours by train.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. The stadium is small, so tickets are scarce and many go to members.

## The city
The wide sandy beach, the pier, the Lower Gardens and the Russell-Cotes Museum. The Jurassic Coast with Durdle Door and Brownsea Island are nearby.

## Why a visit is worth it
Bournemouth is one of the most attractive and smallest Premier League clubs, on the south coast with beach and nature close by. The match is intimate and the atmosphere friendly. The Jurassic Coast and the pier make it a good combination with a day out. Tickets are hard to get because of the small stadium, but those who succeed have a special day.
