## The stadium
The RCDE Stadium (about 40,000 places), also known by the sponsor name Stage Front Stadium, opened in 2009 in Cornellà de Llobregat, just outside Barcelona. It is a modern, functional stadium with covered stands and good sightlines from almost every spot. The stands sit close to the pitch and the design is relatively simple and practical. Around the stadium there is plenty of space for parking and food and drink.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Espanyol is proud, rebellious and outspoken: the supporters see themselves as the true Barcelona fans who do not go along with the masses. The crowd sings, waves flags and stays loyal to its club, even when things go badly. In a match against FC Barcelona the stadium boils, and the crowd turns it into a battle.

## Getting there
By metro line 5 or the tram to Cornellà; the stadium is about a 15 to 20 minute walk from the stop. From the centre it is about 25 to 30 minutes.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get; the Barcelona derby is the hardest.

## The city
Barcelona: Sagrada Família, Park Güell, the Gothic Quarter, Montjuïc and Barceloneta beach.

## Why a visit is worth it
Espanyol is a good alternative for anyone who wants to visit Barcelona without the prices and crowds of Barça. Tickets are cheaper, the stadium is easy to find your way around and the atmosphere is real and local. You are in the middle of Catalan football culture and see a different side of the city. Because you are staying in Barcelona, you can effortlessly combine the match with sights, beach and excellent food.
