## The stadium
The Mewa Arena (about 33,000 places) is in the southwest of Mainz, in the Bretzenheim district, and opened in 2011 to replace the old Bruchweg Stadium. It is a compact, modern stadium with steep stands close to the pitch. The colours red and white dominate and distances are short. The stadium feels easy to navigate and accessible.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Mainz is cheerful, friendly and carnival-like. The crowd sings, claps and enjoys itself, without the grimness of some other German stadiums. It is a good place for anyone visiting a Bundesliga match for the first time.

## Getting there
Take tram 50 or 51 from the main station (Hbf), or walk in about 30 minutes. Frankfurt Airport is about 30 minutes away.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except against Bayern, Dortmund and the derbies against Frankfurt.

## The city
The cathedral, the Gutenberg Museum (the inventor of printing lived here), the Chagall windows in St. Stephan, the Rhine promenade and wine regions nearby. The Mainz carnival is famous.

## Why a visit is worth it
Mainz 05 is a likeable club with a pleasant stadium and a friendly atmosphere. Tickets are generally easy to get, the stadium is easy to reach and matchday is relaxed. The city itself has a beautiful cathedral, the Gutenberg Museum and the Rhine, and lies in the middle of a wine region. If you want to combine football with wine and culture, this is an excellent choice.
