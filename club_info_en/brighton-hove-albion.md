## The stadium
The Amex Stadium (about 31,800 places) opened in 2011 in Falmer, northeast of Brighton, at the foot of the South Downs. The stadium is modern and has a curved, green and white facade that fits well in the landscape. The stands are covered and the view is excellent. Falmer station is right next to the stadium.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Brighton is friendly, cheerful and proud. The crowd sings a lot and is happy with the club's success. Thanks to its green setting, matchday feels relaxed, far from the bustle of the city.

## Getting there
Take the train from Brighton station to Falmer; that is about 10 minutes and the station is right next to the stadium. From London it is about 1 hour by train.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Mainly members get access, so it is difficult, except sometimes for lower-tier cup matches.

## The city
The Royal Pavilion, Brighton Pier, The Lanes, the pebble beach and the nearby chalk cliffs of the Seven Sisters.

## Why a visit is worth it
Brighton is one of the nicest seaside towns in England and an easy day trip from London. The club plays modern, attractive football in a beautiful stadium. The city has a pier, a pebble beach, a remarkable palace and a lively nightlife. Anyone who wants to combine football with beach and atmosphere will find an ideal destination here.
