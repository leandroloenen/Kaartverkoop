## The stadium
Estadio Benito Villamarín (about 60,000 places) is in the south of Seville and is a large, green and white stadium with a spacious layout. The stands are high and sit close to the pitch, and the stadium has been extensively modernised. You see Betis's green and white stripes everywhere, from the seats to the surroundings. The stadium has the look of a large, festive meeting point.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Betis is festive, musical and involved: the crowd sings along to the club's anthem and keeps supporting its team, even when things go badly. The Benito Villamarín is known for its atmosphere, and on matchdays the whole area is green and white. It is an exceptionally warm and exuberant football party.

## Getting there
The stadium is in the south of the city, about 4 km from the centre. You take a bus or taxi, or walk in about an hour. The airport is on the northeast side.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. For most matches this is achievable, but for the Gran Derbi against Sevilla tickets are very hard to get.

## The city
See Seville under Sevilla FC: the Alcázar, the cathedral with the Giralda, Plaza de España and the Triana and Santa Cruz districts.

## Why a visit is worth it
Real Betis has one of the most loyal and exuberant followings in Spain and a stadium full of atmosphere. You see a team with attractive football and a crowd that loves its club in good times and bad. Seville itself offers culture, tapas, flamenco and a sunny climate. Anyone who wants to experience southern Spanish football in its purest form should not skip Betis.
