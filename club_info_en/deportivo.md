## The stadium
Estadio de Riazor (about 32,000 places) is right on the Atlantic coast of A Coruña, next to the Riazor and Orzán beaches. It is a classic stadium with an oval layout that you can see from afar by the ocean. From certain stands you can smell the sea and almost look out over the water. It is one of the most beautifully located stadiums in Spain and has its own nostalgic character.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Deportivo is warm and loud, with the Riazor Blues behind the goal, one of the best-known supporters' groups in Spain. The crowd sings a lot and persistently, even when the club plays in the lower divisions. Around the stadium you can eat and drink well before and after the match, close to the coast.

## Getting there
The stadium is in the city, a 20 to 30 minute walk from the centre. Alvedro airport is about 10 km away; Santiago de Compostela is an alternative.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Generally easy to get. The fanatical Riazor Blues stand behind the goal.

## The city
A Coruña has the Tower of Hercules, a Roman lighthouse and UNESCO World Heritage Site, the Paseo Marítimo, Plaza de María Pita and a well-known seafood scene.

## Why a visit is worth it
Deportivo A Coruña offers an authentic, less touristy side of Spanish football in a unique stadium by the sea. The club has a loyal following and a special atmosphere, without the crowds and prices of the big Spanish clubs. A Coruña is also a beautiful coastal city with a remarkable lighthouse, lovely promenades and excellent seafood. Anyone who wants to explore Galicia and also see football has the ideal reason here.
