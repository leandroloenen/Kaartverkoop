## The stadium
The MAC³PARK stadion (formerly Oosterenk, about 14,000 places) is a compact, modern stadium on the east side of Zwolle, close to the IJssel. The four stands sit close to the pitch, so you have a good view even from the cheaper seats. The stadium has a low, clear layout with plenty of room for food and drink and a friendly atmosphere around the match.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at PEC Zwolle is familial and involved, with many supporters who have known each other for years. The Blauwvingers (the club's nickname) create a close atmosphere in the supporters' section and the crowd gets involved. The stadium is not so big, so as a visitor you are quickly drawn into the experience.

## Getting there
The stadium is on the east side of the city, a few kilometres from the station. Many visitors come by bike or car; from the station you can also walk or take a bus.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except against the big clubs and in derbies.

## The city
Zwolle is a Hanseatic city with a well-preserved centre: the Sassenpoort, the Thorbeckegracht, the Grote Kerk and Museum de Fundatie, with the striking "egg" on its roof.

## Why a visit is worth it
PEC Zwolle is a club with its own relaxed character, far from big-city bustle. You sit close to the play, can easily get tickets and are back in the centre after a short bike ride. Zwolle itself is one of the most beautiful Hanseatic cities in the Netherlands, with a well-preserved centre and a buzzing hospitality scene. Anyone looking for an affordable, pleasant matchday with a city to stroll through is well placed here.
