## The stadium
The Weserstadion (about 42,000 places) is right on the River Weser and within walking distance of the centre of Bremen. The stadium has been rebuilt several times, but has kept its classic character, with green and white accents and stands close to the pitch. It sits in a park along the water, so you can have a nice walk before or after the match. The surroundings are green and the atmosphere relaxed.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Werder is warm and loyal, with a crowd that has followed its club for generations. The Ostkurve behind the goal provides singing, flags and a solid atmosphere. It is a typical northern German matchday: down to earth, friendly, but full of passion at important matches.

## Getting there
Take tram 2 or 10 from the Hbf to the Weserstadion stop, or walk in about 20 minutes.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except for Bayern, Dortmund and the North derby against Hamburg.

## The city
The Marktplatz with the town hall and the Roland (UNESCO), the statue of the Town Musicians of Bremen, the Schnoorviertel and the Böttcherstraße.

## Why a visit is worth it
Werder Bremen is one of the traditional clubs of the Bundesliga and has a stadium with plenty of atmosphere, in the middle of a pleasant city. Tickets are generally affordable and matchday feels authentic. Bremen is compact, beautiful and friendly, and offers unique sights such as the Roland and the Town Musicians of Bremen. Anyone looking for a small-scale German football weekend is in the right place here.
