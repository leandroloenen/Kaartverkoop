## The stadium
The City Ground (about 30,000 places) is on the south bank of the River Trent, just outside the centre of Nottingham. It is a classic English stadium with a low, clear layout and a view of the water from some spots. Forest's red colours are everywhere. The stadium has a nostalgic atmosphere and suits the club's history well.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Forest is proud and loud: the crowd sings a lot and the mood at important matches is particularly intense. The pubs along the river are full before the match. It is an authentic English matchday with plenty of tradition.

## Getting there
From Nottingham station you walk to the stadium in about 15 minutes across the river.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Many places are for members; tickets are hard to get, especially for top matches and the derby against Derby County.

## The city
Nottingham Castle, Old Market Square, the Lace Market, Ye Olde Trip to Jerusalem (one of the oldest pubs in England), Wollaton Hall and Sherwood Forest, known from Robin Hood.

## Why a visit is worth it
Nottingham Forest is a club with a special place in European football history and a stadium with plenty of character. The location by the river is beautiful, the crowd proud and the atmosphere authentic. Nottingham offers castles, historic pubs and the legend of Robin Hood. Anyone who wants to see a classic English match is in the right place here, although tickets are hard to get.
