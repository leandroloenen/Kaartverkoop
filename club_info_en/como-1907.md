## The stadium
The Stadio Giuseppe Sinigaglia (about 13,600 places) is right on Lake Como, with a view of the mountains. The stadium has a historic design from the 1930s and a characteristic low layout with a special arch. Behind one goal you see the water and the surrounding hills. It is one of the most beautifully located stadiums in Europe.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Como is special: a small stadium, an enthusiastic crowd and a view of the lake. The match feels intimate and almost festive. Before the match you can walk along the shore and have an aperitivo.

## Getting there
Take the train from Milano Centrale or Cadorna to Como San Giovanni or Como Lago, about 40 minutes. The stadium is about a 10 minute walk from the centre.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. The stadium is small and demand has grown, so tickets are scarce. Book well in advance.

## The city
Lake Como, the Duomo, the funicular to Brunate, Villa Olmo and boat trips to Bellagio and Varenna.

## Why a visit is worth it
Como offers a unique combination of Serie A football and one of the most beautiful lakes in Europe. The stadium is small and picturesque, and the town has a charming old centre and a funicular up into the mountains. Tickets are scarce, so plan ahead. Anyone looking for a special football trip will find a rare combination here.
