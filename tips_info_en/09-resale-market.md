# Buying tickets on a club's official resale platform: the smart way into a sold-out match

Many fans assume a match is gone the moment general sale closes. That is not always true. More and more clubs run their own marketplace or resale platform where season ticket holders can offer their seat when they cannot attend. It is one of the most underrated ways to get a ticket, and it is fully official.

## How does it work?

A season ticket holder who cannot make a game puts the ticket back on sale through the club. You buy it at face value (sometimes plus a small service fee), and the ticket is reissued or transferred to your name. You pay face value, and you know the ticket is genuine.

This is very common in Germany, where many Bundesliga clubs have such a platform. It is also growing in the Netherlands, England and other countries. I have bought tickets several times myself via Ajax's resale platform.

## When do you stand a chance?

Be realistic: the biggest games rarely show up. Do not count on finding a ticket for Borussia Dortmund against Bayern Munich, and the same goes for Ajax against Feyenoord. Season ticket holders use those seats themselves or pass them on to family and friends.

But for the many other fixtures, often against less glamorous opponents, it is a genuine opportunity. If you simply want to experience a great atmosphere in a big stadium once, this is often the easiest route.

## The best time to look

Most tickets appear in the final two weeks before the match. That is when life gets in the way: people fall ill, have other plans or have to work. So do not only check right after the announcement. Keep coming back, because tickets can still appear in the last few days and sometimes even on matchday.

## Tips

- Look on the club's website for terms such as *resale*, *ticket exchange*, *marketplace* or *Zweitmarkt*.
- Create an account in advance so you can order quickly. Good tickets often go within minutes.
- Check the page regularly, or set yourself a reminder.
- Preferably buy through the club's official platform. There you know the ticket is valid.
- Read the terms: some platforms require an account or membership, and not every club allows every match.

On Football Ticket Scout you can see when general sale opens for each club. If a match is sold out, the resale market is your second chance.
