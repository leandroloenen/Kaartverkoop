## The stadium
The MHPArena (about 60,000 places), the former Neckarstadion, is in Bad Cannstatt, on the bank of the Neckar. The stadium was extensively rebuilt from an athletics stadium into a real football arena, with stands close to the pitch. The roof holds the sound in and gives the stadium an intense atmosphere. It sits in a sports and leisure area with plenty of greenery.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Stuttgart is fierce, loyal and loud, with the Cannstatter Kurve as the heart of the supporters' section. The crowd supports the club through thick and thin, and at important matches the stadium is a seething mass of white and red. Before and after the match it is lively at the stalls around the stadium.

## Getting there
Take the S-Bahn S1 from the Hbf to Neckarpark (Stadion), about 10 to 15 minutes.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Many places are for members and recently demand has been high; plan early.

## The city
The Mercedes-Benz Museum, the Porsche Museum, the Schlossplatz, the Wilhelma zoo and the surrounding vineyards. In autumn there is the Cannstatter Volksfest.

## Why a visit is worth it
VfB Stuttgart has a large and loyal crowd in a stadium that offers plenty of atmosphere. The city is special for those who love cars, with museums of Mercedes-Benz and Porsche, and also offers vineyards and parks. It is one of the more underrated destinations for a football trip in Germany. Anyone who wants to see a big club without the tourist crowds of Berlin or Munich is well placed here.
