## The stadium
The Bluenergy Stadium, also known as Stadio Friuli (about 25,000 places), is in the north of Udine, within walking distance of the centre. The stadium was extensively renovated in 2016 and now has four covered stands close to the pitch. The club's black and white colours dominate and the surroundings are green and quiet. The stadium is modern, comfortable and easy to find your way around.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Udinese is friendly, welcoming and proud. The crowd sings a lot and is loyal to its club, without the grimness of some other stadiums. It is a relaxed way to experience Serie A, in a quiet region.

## Getting there
From Udine station you walk to the stadium in about 20 minutes, or take a bus. Venice is about 2 hours away by train.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get.

## The city
Piazza della Libertà, Piazza Matteotti, Udine castle and the wine regions of Friuli. Try the local white wine and the frico (a cheese dish). Trieste and Venice are close by.

## Why a visit is worth it
Udinese offers a quiet and affordable match in a part of Italy that attracts few tourists. The stadium is modern and the atmosphere warm. Udine has a beautiful centre, good wines and its own cuisine, and Venice and Trieste are close. Anyone who wants to see the real Italy, outside the big cities, is well placed here.
