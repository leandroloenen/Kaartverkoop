## The stadium
The Gtech Community Stadium (about 17,000 places) opened in 2020 and is on the west side of London, close to the Thames and Kew Bridge. The stadium is compact and modern, with steep stands and an excellent view. The special design with flats around it makes it part of the neighbourhood. The old Griffin Park, with pubs on all four corners, is a thing of the past.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Brentford is friendly, welcoming and local. The crowd sings a lot and the match feels like a neighbourhood party. Before the match you can go to the pubs in the area, where the mood is often lively.

## Getting there
From Kew Bridge or Brentford station it is about a 10 to 15 minute walk. From London Waterloo it is about 20 minutes by train.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. The stadium is small, so it is difficult; members come first.

## The city
Kew Gardens (UNESCO), Syon Park, the Thames and of course all of London a stone's throw away.

## Why a visit is worth it
Brentford offers a small and friendly alternative to the big London clubs, with modern football in a compact stadium. The stadium is close to the centre, and Kew Gardens and the Thames are nearby. The match is relaxed and you sit close to the pitch. Anyone looking for a pleasant afternoon will be well served here.
