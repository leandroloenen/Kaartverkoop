## The stadium
Old Trafford (about 74,000 places), "The Theatre of Dreams", is the largest club stadium in England and is in Stretford, on the southwest side of Manchester. The stadium has tall stands that enclose the pitch and its own, almost monumental look. There is a large club museum and a tour in which you also see the players' tunnel and the dressing rooms. There are plans for a new stadium, so check the current situation.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Old Trafford is grand: the crowd sings, the red scarves go up and expectations are always high. Around the stadium are pubs, stalls and an enormous fan shop. It is a matchday you do not quickly forget, even though it is not always equally loud inside the stadium itself.

## Getting there
By tram (Metrolink) from Manchester Piccadilly or Victoria to Old Trafford, or by train to Manchester United Football Ground on matchdays. Allow about 20 minutes.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Very difficult: season tickets are taken and single tickets are scarce. Tours and the museum are a good alternative.

## The city
The National Football Museum, the Northern Quarter, John Rylands Library, the Town Hall and Salford Quays with the Imperial War Museum North.

## Why a visit is worth it
Manchester United is one of the best-known football clubs in the world and Old Trafford is a place of pilgrimage for football fans. The stadium is imposing and a tour is a sight in itself. Manchester also offers a rich football and music culture, museums and a pleasant centre. Anyone who wants to experience a piece of football history cannot go around Old Trafford.
