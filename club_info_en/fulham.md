## The stadium
Craven Cottage (about 29,000 places) is on the bank of the Thames in southwest London, in the middle of a park. The stadium has a characteristic white wooden pavilion in the corner, which is a protected monument. The stands are close to the pitch and the surroundings are green and quiet. It is one of the most beautiful and special stadiums in England.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Fulham is friendly, relaxed and genuinely British. The crowd sings, but the mood is easygoing and hospitable. Before the match you stroll through Bishop's Park along the river and have a pint in one of the pubs.

## Getting there
Take the tube (District Line) to Putney Bridge and walk about 15 minutes through Bishop's Park to the stadium.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Somewhat easier than at the bigger London clubs, but many matches sell out quickly.

## The city
Bishop's Park, Fulham Palace, the Thames Path and the pubs by the river. Central London is close by.

## Why a visit is worth it
Fulham offers one of the most special stadium experiences in England, by the water and with plenty of history. The crowd is friendly and matchday is relaxed. London lies at your feet, so you can combine the visit with museums, parks and the river. Anyone who wants to see a classic English stadium without the biggest crowds is well placed here.
