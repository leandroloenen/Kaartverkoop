## The stadium
The Coventry Building Society Arena (about 32,600 places) opened in 2005 in the north of Coventry and is right next to its own railway station. The stadium has a modern design with four covered stands and a round, clear layout. It is also used for concerts and other events. The club's sky-blue colours are everywhere.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Coventry City is friendly, hopeful and loud. The crowd is happy with the revived success and sings along enthusiastically. The organisation is good and the stadium is easy to reach, which makes matchday relaxed.

## Getting there
Take the train from Coventry to Coventry Arena, in about 5 minutes. From Birmingham or London, Coventry can be reached in about 1 hour.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get.

## The city
Coventry Cathedral (the ruins and the new cathedral), the Coventry Transport Museum, the statue of Lady Godiva and the Herbert Art Gallery. The city was UK City of Culture in 2021.

## Why a visit is worth it
Coventry City offers an accessible and affordable English matchday experience in a modern stadium with its own railway station. The crowd is friendly and the atmosphere easygoing. Coventry has a remarkable cathedral, a transport museum and a rich history. For anyone who wants to combine cheap English football with a stop in the Midlands, this is a good choice.
