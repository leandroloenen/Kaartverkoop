## The stadium
The Volksparkstadion is in the west of Hamburg, in the Altona part of the city, and is a large stadium with steep stands. It is easy to reach by public transport. For many matches, sales start with members first, and tickets for the big matches go quickly. Check the club in good time.

## The matchday experience
HSV is a historic club with a large, loyal following, and the atmosphere at the Volksparkstadion is loud and passionate. The derby against St. Pauli gives Hamburg a city derby that is followed across Germany. Combine your visit with a day in the city and the harbour.
