## The stadium
The Europa-Park Stadion (about 34,700 places) opened in 2021 and is in the northwest of Freiburg, next to the airfield and the university campus. The stadium is modern and sustainably built, with plenty of wood in the construction and a large roof with solar panels. The stands are steep and close to the pitch. The stadium fits the green character of the city.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Freiburg is friendly, involved and down to earth. The crowd is proud of the club and supports the team with lots of singing, but without aggression. It is one of the most pleasant and accessible stadiums in which to visit a Bundesliga match.

## Getting there
By tram or shuttle bus from the centre. Allow about 25 to 30 minutes from the Hbf. Check the current route on the official club site.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually doable, but harder for Bayern and Dortmund.

## The city
The Gothic Münster, the Bächle (little streams of water in the streets), the Schlossberg, the Augustinermuseum and the Black Forest on the doorstep. Freiburg is one of the sunniest cities in Germany.

## Why a visit is worth it
SC Freiburg is a likeable club with a modern stadium and a pleasant atmosphere. Freiburg is one of the most beautiful and sunniest cities in Germany, with a Gothic cathedral, a lively centre and the Black Forest around the corner. The match is a good addition to a trip through southwest Germany. Anyone looking for a friendly match in a beautiful city chooses well here.
