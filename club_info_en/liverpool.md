## The stadium
Anfield (about 61,000 places) is in a residential area in the north of Liverpool and is one of the best-known stadiums in the world. The Kop, the famous standing terrace, is now a seated stand where some 12,000 supporters sit behind one goal. Around the stadium are monuments, such as the Shankly Gates and the Hillsborough Memorial. There is a club museum and you can take a tour.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Anfield is legendary: before kick-off the whole stadium sings "You'll Never Walk Alone" with scarves held overhead. On European nights the stadium is one of the most impressive places in football. The crowd is proud and critical, and the experience is emotional.

## Getting there
From Liverpool Lime Street you take the bus (about 20 minutes) or walk in about an hour. There is no railway station right by the stadium.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Very difficult: season tickets and memberships come first. A tour and museum are a good alternative.

## The city
Albert Dock, The Beatles Story, the Cavern Club, the Royal Liver Building, the cathedrals and the Mersey Ferry.

## Why a visit is worth it
Liverpool is one of the big names in football and Anfield is a stadium you must have visited at least once. The atmosphere is incomparable and the club is interwoven with the city. Liverpool itself offers the Beatles, Albert Dock and a rich maritime history. Tickets are very hard to get, but even without a match a tour is worth it.
