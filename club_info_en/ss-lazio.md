## The stadium
The Stadio Olimpico (about 70,000 places) is Lazio's home ground and is in the Foro Italico, on the north side of Rome. The stadium was built for the 1960 Olympic Games and has a large, oval layout with an athletics track. It is shared with AS Roma. Around the stadium are marble statues and sports grounds from the time of the fascist regime, which gives a special atmosphere.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Lazio is fierce, proud and sky blue: the Curva Nord sings and waves flags throughout the match. The supporters are known for their fierce, sometimes controversial support. It is an intense experience with plenty of emotion, especially in the Derby della Capitale.

## Getting there
Take metro line A to Flaminio and then tram 2 or a bus. On matchdays it is very busy; allow about 30 to 40 minutes from the centre.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, but the Derby della Capitale against Roma and the matches against Inter, Juventus and Milan are difficult. ID is required.

## The city
See Rome: the Colosseum, the Roman Forum, the Pantheon, the Trevi Fountain and the Vatican.

## Why a visit is worth it
Lazio offers an authentic Roman matchday experience with a loyal and passionate crowd. The stadium is large and the atmosphere intense. Rome itself is a dream city with history, art and food. For anyone who wants to see Roman football culture from both sides, a visit to Lazio is a nice addition.
