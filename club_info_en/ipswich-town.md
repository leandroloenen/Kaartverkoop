## The stadium
Portman Road (about 30,000 places) is in the middle of the centre of Ipswich, within walking distance of the station. It is a classic English stadium with four covered stands, some of which are modern and others more old-fashioned. The stands are close to the pitch and the blue and white colours dominate the view. In front of the entrance are statues of two legendary managers.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Ipswich is friendly, proud and warm. The crowd sings a lot and is happy with the return to the top level. It is a relaxed and friendly matchday, without the aggression of some other stadiums.

## Getting there
From Ipswich station you walk in about 10 minutes. From London Liverpool Street it is about 1 hour and 10 minutes by train.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except for bigger matches and the derby against Norwich.

## The city
Christchurch Mansion, the Waterfront, the Cardinal Wolsey heritage and the Suffolk coast, for example Southwold or Aldeburgh.

## Why a visit is worth it
Ipswich Town offers an old-fashioned and accessible English football day in a stadium in the middle of the town. Tickets are generally easy to get and the atmosphere is warm. The town lies in a beautiful region with the Suffolk coast and small villages within reach. Anyone who wants to experience English football quietly, without the bustle of London, is in the right place here.
