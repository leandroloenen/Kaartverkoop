## The stadium
The AFAS Stadion (about 19,500 places) opened in 2006 and is on the edge of Alkmaar, in a new-build district with plenty of space. It is a modern stadium with steep stands close to the pitch, so the sound builds up well. Typical are the club's red and white accents and the clear layout. There are enough food and drink outlets, so you can eat and drink well before kick-off.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at AZ is friendly and involved, with a loyal crowd and an enthusiastic section behind the goal. Matchday is pleasantly organised and the stadium is easy to find your way around, which makes it ideal for a first visit to an Eredivisie match. On European nights and in matches against the big clubs the experience is a lot more intense.

## Getting there
From Alkmaar Centraal the stadium can be reached by bus or on foot, a few kilometres. On matchdays extra buses run. By car you can park at the stadium, but it fills up quickly.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Tickets are usually easy to get, except against Ajax, PSV, Feyenoord and for European matches. Book those in good time.

## The city
Alkmaar is a pleasant, historic city with canals. The famous cheese market on the Waagplein takes place on Fridays in the summer months. Also visit the Dutch Beer Museum, the Grote Kerk and the Stedelijk Museum Alkmaar.

## Why a visit is worth it
AZ is one of the best-organised clubs in the Netherlands with a modern, comfortable stadium and attractive football. Alkmaar is a pleasant, compact city that you can easily explore on foot. The cheese market, the canals and the atmospheric shopping streets make it a nice day trip. For anyone who wants to combine a relaxed match with a visit to a typical Dutch town, this is a good choice.
