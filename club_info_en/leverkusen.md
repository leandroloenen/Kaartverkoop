## The stadium
The BayArena (about 30,000 places) is Leverkusen's home ground and sits on the edge of the centre, next to the Bayer company site. It is a compact and modern stadium with four covered stands close to the pitch. The stadium has been fully modernised and offers a good view and comfort. You see the club's red and black colours everywhere.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Leverkusen is friendly, well organised and increasingly enthusiastic since the championship. The crowd is proud of its team and supports it loudly. Distances are short, which makes for a pleasant matchday.

## Getting there
Leverkusen lies right next to Cologne. Take the train to Leverkusen Mitte and walk (about 20 minutes) or take the bus. From Cologne you are there in about 30 minutes.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Since the title demand has been high; for Bayern, Dortmund and European top matches it is very difficult.

## The city
Leverkusen itself is modest: Schloss Morsbroich and the Neulandpark. Combine the visit with Cologne, Düsseldorf or Altenberg Cathedral.

## Why a visit is worth it
Bayer Leverkusen is a club that shows it can be done: after decades of near misses it became national champion. The stadium is compact, modern and comfortable and the football attractive. Leverkusen itself is modest, but it lies next to Cologne and Düsseldorf, so you can quickly make a nice weekend of it. For anyone who wants to see the current German top in a friendly setting, this is an excellent choice.
