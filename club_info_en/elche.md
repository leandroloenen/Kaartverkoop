## The stadium
Estadio Martínez Valero (about 31,000 places) is on the edge of Elche, in an area with plenty of space and parking. It is a large, spacious stadium with a somewhat old-fashioned look and wide stands. The sun is often out, so you regularly sit in the open air with a view of the low buildings. The stadium is not an architectural highlight, but it has its own charm and plenty of space.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Elche is relaxed, friendly and local. The crowd sings along, but it is less hectic than at the big clubs. You see many families and friends and the match suits a sunny afternoon on the Costa Blanca.

## Getting there
Elche is about 20 km from Alicante. You come by train or bus from Alicante and Alicante-Elche airport is close by. The stadium is about 3 km from the centre; a bus or taxi is handy.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, even for bigger matches it is often possible.

## The city
Elche is known for the Palmeral, a UNESCO palm grove, and the Misteri d'Elx, a medieval mystery play, also UNESCO heritage. You can also see the Basílica de Santa María and the Huerto del Cura.

## Why a visit is worth it
Elche is a good choice for anyone visiting the Costa Blanca who would like to catch a match. Tickets are cheap, the stadium is spacious and the atmosphere relaxed. The city has a beautiful palm grove and a rich history, and the beaches of Alicante are close by. So you can easily combine beach, culture and football in a single trip.
