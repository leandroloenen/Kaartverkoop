## The stadium
The Koning Willem II Stadion (about 14,500 places) is on the south side of Tilburg, close to the centre. It is a compact, classic stadium with covered stands close to the pitch, so you have a good view everywhere. The club's red, white and blue colours dominate the view. The stadium is clear and friendly, with enough food and drink outlets and a fan shop.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Willem II is warm, Brabant and proud. The crowd sings a lot and feels every moment, and the supporters are known for their loyalty, even in less good times. The stadium is not too big, so as a visitor you are quickly drawn into the experience.

## Getting there
The stadium is about 2 to 3 km from Tilburg station. You walk there in about half an hour, take the bus or cycle. By car you can park nearby, but it is busy on matchdays.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Usually easy to get, except against the big clubs and in the Brabant derbies. Book in good time then.

## The city
Tilburg has the TextielMuseum, museum De Pont, the Piusplein and the Spoorzone with plenty of restaurants and culture. The Efteling is close by, in Kaatsheuvel.

## Why a visit is worth it
Willem II is a club with its own atmosphere and a loyal following in the heart of Brabant. The stadium is friendly, tickets are generally affordable and the football often attractive. Tilburg is a city with an industrial past that has been transformed into a creative and pleasant city, with museums and a lively Spoorzone. Anyone who wants to combine a relaxed match with a day in Brabant is well placed here.
