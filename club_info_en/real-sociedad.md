## The stadium
The Reale Arena (about 39,500 places), known as Anoeta until 2019, is in the east of San Sebastián, close to the Urumea river. The stadium has been modernised and since the rebuild has a roof, stands close to the pitch and pleasant acoustics. It is a stadium where you have a good view from almost every spot. The club's blue and white stripes are found in and around the arena.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Real Sociedad is proud and musical, with supporters who sing a lot and support their club with almost familial warmth. At the Basque derby with Athletic Club the city goes wild and the tension is tangible in every street. Because many players come from the region, the bond between team and crowd is close.

## Getting there
The stadium is in the east of the city. From the centre you walk there in about 30 minutes or take the bus. On matchdays there are extra services.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. Many places are for season ticket holders, so it is moderately to very difficult; for the Basque derby, Madrid and Barcelona it is very hard.

## The city
Playa de La Concha, the old town (Parte Vieja) with pintxos, Monte Igueldo and Monte Urgull. San Sebastián is known for its gastronomy.

## Why a visit is worth it
Real Sociedad combines good football at a high level with one of the most attractive cities in Spain. The stadium is modern and the crowd enthusiastic, without the overwhelming crowds of the biggest clubs. San Sebastián is famous for its beaches, its pintxos bars and its extensive food culture. You can lie on the beach there in the morning, explore the city in the afternoon and attend a match in the evening: a rare combination.
