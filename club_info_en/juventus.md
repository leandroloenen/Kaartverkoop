## The stadium
The Allianz Stadium (about 41,500 places) opened in 2011 on the site of the old Delle Alpi, in the northwest of Turin. It is one of the first fully club-owned stadiums in Italy and has four steep stands without an athletics track, so you sit close to the pitch. Next to the stadium are the J Museum and a shopping centre. The black and white colours are everywhere.

## The matchday experience
The atmosphere at Juventus is big and professional: the stadium is almost always full and the crowd demands victory. The Curva Sud sings throughout the match and the stadium is well organised. Before the match there are shops, restaurants and a museum, so you can easily spend half a day there.

## Getting there
The stadium is in the northwest of the city. From Porta Nuova or Porta Susa you can take a bus or tram, about 30 to 40 minutes. On matchdays there are extra services.

## Buying tickets
Through the official club site. For the derby against Torino, Inter and Milan difficult; for other matches often more achievable.

## The city
See Turin: Turin has the Mole Antonelliana with the Cinema Museum, the Egyptian Museum, the Royal Palace and the Piazza San Carlo.

## Why a visit is worth it
Juventus is the most successful club in Italy and plays in a modern and comfortable stadium. The match is usually of a high standard and the museum and tour make it a complete programme. Turin is an elegant, quiet city with plenty of culture and excellent food. Anyone who wants to see an Italian top club in a modern stadium will find a good choice here.
